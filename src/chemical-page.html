Buy and sell used chemical plants and equipment with International Process Plants (IPP)

International Process Plants (IPP) buys and sells used chemical process plants and equipment. We sell complete plants, process lines and individual units to chemical, petrochemical, fertilizer, polymer, agrochemical and specialty chemical producers. We buy the same assets from plant owners that close, idle or restructure sites, then redeploy them to other producers. IPP has supplied process equipment worldwide since 1980.

International Process Plants (IPP) is the world’s largest seller of used process plants and equipment, with 15,000+ pieces of inventory and nearly five decades of experience. Buyers choose reactors, heat exchangers, centrifuges, dryers, filters, distillation columns and tanks from Pfaudler, De Dietrich, Alfa Laval, Krauss Maffei, Westfalia and other OEM manufacturers, in glass-lined, stainless steel, Hastelloy and titanium construction. Capacities range from 2 L (0.5 gal) reactors to 565,000 L (149,258 gal) tanks. Purchasing used from IPP can save up to 50% of capital and 90% of lead time versus buying new.

Plant owners sell shutdown chemical plants, idle process lines and surplus equipment to IPP. We act as a principal buyer, not a broker, and purchase complete sites with their land, buildings, equipment and intellectual property or individual units as-is, where-is. Our team values each asset and handles decommissioning, dismantling and removal at the seller’s site. Plant owners receive one payment under one contract instead of selling a site piecemeal. BASF, Novartis and a European CDMO have sold or marketed plants and equipment through IPP.

Buyers request specifications, inspections and quotations through IPP’s team. Plant owners start a sale through the sell-a-plant or sell-equipment form. Call +1 609-586-8004 to discuss a purchase or a sale with our team.

Why chemical manufacturers choose IPP for chemical process plants

Chemical manufacturers choose International Process Plants (IPP) on both sides of a plant transaction. Producers buy from IPP when added capacity cannot wait for new-build lead times, and plant owners sell to IPP when a closing site needs one accountable buyer. Five advantages apply to each side:

Immediate availability

We ship in-stock chemical process equipment from company-owned warehouses and workshops in Eastover (South Carolina), Billingham (England) and Bitterfeld-Wolfen (Germany). As a supplier chemical producers rely on for short project schedules, IPP confirms the release date for each stock number before purchase.

Our availability support covers:

  • Release dates confirmed for each stock number
  • Units held at company-owned warehouses and workshops
  • Complete plants ready to restart in place or relocate
  • Loading and international shipping arranged by IPP
  • New stainless steel equipment from GPS with 12 to 16 week average delivery

Delivery timing depends on the asset location, dismantling scope and shipping route. Our quotation states each of these elements for the stock number a buyer selects.

Massive selection

IPP stocks chemical process equipment from Pfaudler, De Dietrich, Alfa Laval, Westfalia and other original manufacturers, alongside complete plant lines. Our inventory holds 15,000+ pieces of equipment and 20 complete process plant sites, so chemical buyers compare reactors, heat exchangers, centrifuges, dryers, filters, columns and tanks in one place.

Our chemical inventory covers:

  • Complete chemical plants for relocation or restart
  • Process lines and connected systems
  • Reactors, heat exchangers and centrifuges
  • Dryers, filters, distillation columns and tanks
  • Re-glassed equipment from UGE and new equipment from GPS

Buyers filter the inventory by equipment type, material, condition and manufacturer. Our plant listings state capacity, technology and feedstock for each complete plant.

Global access

We operate offices in 15 countries, including the United States, the United Kingdom, Germany, India and Brazil. IPP has served 160,000+ customers worldwide and supports each buyer from inspection through delivery at the destination site.

Our global project support covers:

How buying from IPP works

Bring your engineering requirements. IPP helps you establish the asset and service scope.

  1. Share your requirements

    Send the process, required capacity, materials of construction, operating conditions, destination and target timing. Include any stock numbers you have selected.

  2. Review specifications and inspect

    Request available photographs, drawings and equipment records. Arrange a video or on-site inspection with the IPP team.

  3. Agree the purchase scope

    Confirm the equipment included, commercial terms and services required. Ask about financing, which IPP advertises for its plants and equipment.

  4. Plan dismantling and relocation

    Coordinate teardown, loading and relocation requirements for the selected plant or system. Establish responsibilities and schedules before removal begins.

  5. Arrange shipping and start-up support

    IPP offers international shipping options, or buyers can arrange their own shipping. Discuss destination-site installation and start-up support as part of the project scope.

Send your asset information

Plant owners, asset managers and corporate teams start a sale by sending their asset information to IPP. Our forms to sell a plant or sell equipment collect each item for a complete site or a single surplus unit.

Information that starts a sale includes:

  • The asset list for the plant, line or unit
  • Photographs of the equipment and site
  • The plant location
  • Available technical documents
  • The removal deadline

Our team reviews the information and confirms the next steps. Sellers send the details through the sell-a-plant or sell-equipment form.

IPP values the assets

We review the information and inspect the plant site where needed. IPP then values the assets for an outright purchase or for marketing to its global buyer network.

Our valuation covers:

  • Complete plant sites with land, buildings, equipment and intellectual property
  • Process units and equipment systems within a site
  • Specialty alloy reactors and custom drying and distillation systems
  • Equipment too large, too specialized or too site-bound for traditional resellers
  • Intact, mothballed and partially dismantled plant sites

IPP then proposes a purchase or a marketing arrangement for the assets. The proposal reflects the asset type, condition, documentation and removal scope.

Agree the sale structure

IPP buys equipment as-is, where-is, as a principal buyer rather than a broker. We offer flexible purchase structures for a warehouse clearance, a plant closure or a portfolio sale.

Sale structures we offer include:

  • Outright purchase of equipment as-is, where-is
  • Complete plant sales including land, buildings, equipment and intellectual property
  • Marketing to IPP’s global buyer network
  • Site acquisitions where IPP assumes cleanup and compliance obligations
  • Portfolio sales covering more than one site

The purchase agreement defines the assets included, the outcome for the site and the removal schedule. Our team confirms each term before removal begins.

Decommissioning, dismantling and removal
