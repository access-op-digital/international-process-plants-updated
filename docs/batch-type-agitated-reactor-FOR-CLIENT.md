**Q: What is the difference between glass-lined and stainless steel batch reactors?**

Glass-lined reactors have a borosilicate glass coating fused to the interior metal surface, providing excellent corrosion resistance for acidic and alkaline processes common in pharmaceutical and fine chemical manufacturing. Stainless steel reactors use bare metal construction (316, 316L, 304, 321 grades) and are suited for processes where the metal alloy provides sufficient corrosion resistance, such as food/beverage, biotech, and many chemical applications. Glass-lined reactors require re-glassing when the lining is damaged, while stainless steel reactors do not have this maintenance requirement.
