Validation-ready

IPP sources equipment from operating plants with known history, including multinational chemical producers, toll manufacturers and restructured facilities. We supply the records available for each stock number, so engineering and quality teams review an asset before purchase.

Records available for review include:

  • Photographs of each unit
  • Equipment drawings
  • Process descriptions for complete plants
  • Inspection records
  • Equipment lists for plants and process lines

The records available differ by asset, and our team confirms them for each stock number. Buyers arrange a video or on-site inspection to verify the unit in person.
