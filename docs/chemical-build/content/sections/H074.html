Capital from idle assets

We turn idle and surplus chemical process equipment into capital for the seller. IPP resells or redeploys the assets to manufacturers worldwide, so the seller recovers value instead of paying for storage and disposal.

Seller value from an IPP sale includes:

  • Capital recovered from unused or surplus assets
  • Long-term storage and maintenance costs removed
  • Decommissioning reserves turned into a positive financial event
  • Assets redeployed rather than scrapped
  • One payment under one contract

The value depends on asset type, condition, documentation and removal scope. IPP proposes an outright purchase or a marketing arrangement for each asset list.
