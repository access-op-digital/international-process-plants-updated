Evaporators

Film, flash and crystallizer evaporators for concentration and solvent removal.

We supply used evaporators to chemical plants that concentrate solutions, recover solvents and crystallize products. As a supplier chemical producers trust for evaporation duty, IPP sources units from Luwa SMS, Buss-SMS-Canzler, Buflovak/Blaw Knox and Pfaudler and records evaporation rate and heat transfer area for each stock number.

Evaporator types in our inventory cover:

  • Rising and falling film evaporators, including multiple-effect systems
  • Wiped and thin film evaporators
  • Flash evaporators
  • Crystallizer evaporators from zero liquid discharge systems
  • Hastelloy C-22 clad and titanium units for corrosive service

Each evaporator listing states evaporation rate, heat transfer area, pressure and temperature. Stainless steel 316 leads our evaporator inventory, alongside stainless steel 304 and 316L, fiberglass, glass-lined steel and titanium, with evaporation rates from 4.5 kg/h to 36,300 kg/h (10 to 80,000 lb/h) and heat transfer areas up to 1,473 m² (15,850 ft²). Pricing depends on manufacturer, evaporation rate, material of construction and condition, and our team quotes each stock number individually.
