Turnkey removal

IPP handles each acquisition from the on-site inspection to dismantling coordination, packaging, freight and export. Our team manages every step, so the seller coordinates one buyer instead of separate vendors.

Our turnkey removal covers:

  • On-site inspection at the seller’s plant
  • Dismantling coordination
  • Packaging and crating
  • Freight and export
  • Removal on the seller’s schedule

IPP buys as-is, where-is and takes ownership of the removal. The purchase agreement sets the removal schedule.
