Risk and liability transfer

IPP acquires idled, retired and shut down chemical plant sites and other industrial sites, including sites with environmental closure obligations. As the buyer, IPP assumes site cleanup and regulatory compliance obligations in those acquisitions.

Obligations IPP can assume include:

  • Site cleanup
  • Regulatory compliance
  • Soil and groundwater remediation planning
  • Environmental closure permitting
  • Coordination with local environmental authorities

The purchase agreement defines which obligations transfer to IPP. IPP’s in-house team and remediation partners evaluate the contamination before the work begins.
