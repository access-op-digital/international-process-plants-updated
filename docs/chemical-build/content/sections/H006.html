Heat exchangers

Shell-and-tube and plate exchangers for thermal control and energy recovery. We supply used heat exchangers from Alfa Laval, Vicarb, Ralph Coidan and Graham to chemical plants for heating, cooling and condensing process streams.

IPP’s used heat exchanger inventory includes:

  • Shell-and-tube heat exchangers
  • Plate-and-frame heat exchangers
  • Spiral heat exchangers
  • Graphite block heat exchangers
  • Welded plate heat exchangers
  • Air fin coolers

Stainless steel 316 and graphite lead the heat exchanger inventory, alongside stainless steel 304 and 316L, carbon steel, titanium and Hastelloy C-276.

Heat transfer areas reach 2,124 m² (22,863 ft²).

Each heat exchanger listing states heat transfer area, shell and tube pressure, temperature and material.
