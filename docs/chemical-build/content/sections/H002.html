Buy and sell used chemical plants and equipment with International Process Plants (IPP)

International Process Plants (IPP) buys and sells used chemical process plants and equipment. We sell complete plants, process lines and individual units to chemical, petrochemical, fertilizer, polymer, agrochemical and specialty chemical producers. We buy the same assets from plant owners that close, idle or restructure sites, then redeploy them to other producers. IPP has supplied process equipment worldwide since 1980.

International Process Plants (IPP) is the world’s largest seller of used process plants and equipment, with 15,000+ pieces of inventory and nearly five decades of experience. Buyers choose reactors, heat exchangers, centrifuges, dryers, filters, distillation columns and tanks from Pfaudler, De Dietrich, Alfa Laval, Krauss Maffei, Westfalia and other OEM manufacturers, in glass-lined, stainless steel, Hastelloy and titanium construction. Capacities range from 2 L (0.5 gal) reactors to 565,000 L (149,258 gal) tanks. Purchasing used from IPP can save up to 50% of capital and 90% of lead time versus buying new.

Plant owners sell shutdown chemical plants, idle process lines and surplus equipment to IPP. We act as a principal buyer, not a broker, and purchase complete sites with their land, buildings, equipment and intellectual property or individual units as-is, where-is. Our team values each asset and handles decommissioning, dismantling and removal at the seller’s site. Plant owners receive one payment under one contract instead of selling a site piecemeal. BASF, Novartis and a European CDMO have sold or marketed plants and equipment through IPP.
