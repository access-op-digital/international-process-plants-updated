Tanks

For storage, blending and transfer. Jacketed and non-jacketed tanks available. IPP supplies used tanks from Pfaudler, De Dietrich, Grundy and Sinclair Stainless Fabrications to chemical plants for raw material storage, blending and product transfer.

IPP’s used tank inventory includes:

  • Glass-lined tanks
  • Stainless steel 304, 316 and 316L tanks
  • Jacketed process tanks
  • Non-jacketed storage tanks
  • Carbon steel and fiberglass tanks

Stainless steel 304 and glass-lined tanks lead the inventory, alongside stainless steel 316 and 316L, carbon steel and fiberglass.

Tank capacities range from 5 L to 565,000 L (1 to 149,258 gal).

Each tank listing states capacity, internal pressure, temperature and material.
