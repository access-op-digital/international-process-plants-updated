Distillation columns

For purification, solvent recovery and fractional separation. IPP supplies used distillation columns from Pfaudler, De Dietrich, Kühni and Schott to chemical plants for solvent recovery and product purification.

IPP’s used column inventory includes:

  • Packed columns
  • Tray columns
  • Combination columns
  • Contactor columns

Glass-lined and stainless steel 316 columns lead the inventory, alongside stainless steel 304, Hastelloy C-276 and graphite.

Column diameters range from 76 mm (3 in) to 8,000 mm (315 in).

Each column listing states diameter, length, pressure and temperature.
