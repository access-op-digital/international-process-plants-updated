One buyer for the whole site

IPP buys complete plant sites, process units and surplus equipment in one transaction, so a seller signs one contract instead of breaking a site up piecemeal. We purchase the land, buildings, equipment and intellectual property when the sale covers the whole site.

A single sale to IPP can cover:

  • Complete plant sites with land and buildings
  • Process units and equipment systems within a site
  • Individual surplus and idled equipment
  • Intellectual property tied to the plant
  • Equipment too large, specialized or site-bound for traditional resellers

Our team confirms the assets included in the purchase agreement. Plant owners start the sale through the sell-a-plant or sell-equipment form.
