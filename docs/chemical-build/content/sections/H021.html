Immediate availability

We ship in-stock chemical process equipment from company-owned warehouses and workshops in Eastover (South Carolina), Billingham (England) and Bitterfeld-Wolfen (Germany). As a supplier chemical producers rely on for short project schedules, IPP confirms the release date for each stock number before purchase.

Our availability support covers:

  • Release dates confirmed for each stock number
  • Units held at company-owned warehouses and workshops
  • Complete plants ready to restart in place or relocate
  • Loading and international shipping arranged by IPP
  • New stainless steel equipment from GPS with 12 to 16 week average delivery

Delivery timing depends on the asset location, dismantling scope and shipping route. Our quotation states each of these elements for the stock number a buyer selects.
