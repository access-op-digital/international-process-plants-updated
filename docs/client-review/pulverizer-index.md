**Q: What is the difference between a jet pulverizer and a hammer pulverizer?**
A: Jet pulverizers use high-velocity compressed gas streams to drive particle-on-particle impact for ultra-fine size reduction without mechanical contact, which protects against contamination and is well suited to heat-sensitive materials. Hammer pulverizers use rotating hammers and a screen to impact-grind feed material; they offer higher throughput at coarser particle sizes and are often used in chemical, mineral, and recycling applications.

A: IPP stocks pulverizers in stainless steel 304 (26 units), carbon steel (23 units), stainless steel other grades (21 units), stainless steel 316 (13 units), stainless steel 321 (9 units), stainless steel 316L (3 units), and austenitic stainless steel (2 units).
