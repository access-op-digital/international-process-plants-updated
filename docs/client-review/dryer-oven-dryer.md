## Convection Drying Ovens vs Vacuum Ovens

A convection oven dries with circulating hot air at atmospheric pressure, while a vacuum oven dries in a sealed, evacuated chamber. The table sets the two designs side by side.

| Factor | Convection Oven | Vacuum Oven |
|-----|-----|-----|
| Heat Transfer | Fans move heated air over the product (forced air convection) | Heat reaches the product by conduction from heated shelves or walls and by radiation |
| Pressure | Atmospheric | Below atmospheric, 0.667 mbar (0.5 torr) operating vacuum on IPP's Barlow Whitney units |
| Atmosphere | Heated air surrounds the product | Little or no air remains in the chamber, which limits oxidation |
| Drying Temperature | Set by the oven temperature | Moisture and solvents evaporate at a lower temperature under vacuum |
| Vapor Handling | Moist air leaves through vents or an exhaust | A vacuum pump draws vapor out of the sealed chamber |
| In IPP's Inventory | Hedinair, Lewco, Amarc, Lytzen and Caltherm ovens | Barlow Whitney E250-VACO units; larger vacuum shelf dryers on [IPP's vacuum shelf dryer page](/process-equipment/dryer/shelf-dryer/) |

**Q: How does a vacuum drying oven work?**
A: A vacuum drying oven heats product on shelves inside a sealed chamber while a vacuum pump draws out the air and vapor. With the pressure reduced, water and solvents boil off at a lower temperature, and the pump carries the vapor out of the chamber. IPP's Barlow Whitney industrial vacuum ovens heat the chamber with electric elements spaced around its exterior and reach an operating vacuum of 0.667 mbar (0.5 torr) through Busch R-5 rotary vane pumps.

**Q: What is a drum warming oven?**
A: Drum warming ovens, also sold as drum heating cabinets or drum ovens, hold sealed drums or IBCs in a heated, insulated chamber until their contents reach temperature. IPP's Hedinair and Lewco units heat with steam, the Lewco to NFPA class A with fire protection, and the Amarc ovens heat electrically and take four 200 liter (53 gal) drums or one 1,000 liter (264 gal) IBC.
