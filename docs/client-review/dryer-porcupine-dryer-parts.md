**Q: How does a Porcupine Processor work?**
A: A Porcupine Processor dries by indirect heat: steam or hot oil flows through the hollow agitator shaft, its rows of hollow paddles and the jacketed trough, so the heating medium never touches the product. As the shaft turns, the paddles mix the material and move it along the trough against the heated surfaces, and the vapor leaves through exhaust ports, under vacuum on some units. The design runs in batch or continuous mode.

**Q: What are the main parts of a porcupine dryer?**
A: A porcupine dryer has three main parts: the jacketed trough that holds the product, the hollow agitator shaft, single or twin, and the wedge-shaped hollow flights, or paddles, mounted along the shaft. Because the heating medium flows through the shaft and paddles as well as the jacket, the agitator adds heat transfer surface relative to the working volume. IPP's inventory holds troughs and a shaft and flights set.
