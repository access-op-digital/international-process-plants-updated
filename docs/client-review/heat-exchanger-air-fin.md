## Air Cooled vs Shell and Tube Heat Exchangers

Air cooled exchangers reject heat to ambient air, while shell and tube exchangers pass it to a second fluid, usually cooling water. Each row below weighs an air cooled unit against a water-cooled shell and tube unit.

| Factor | Air Cooled | Shell and Tube |
|-----|-----|-----|
| Cooling Medium | Ambient air moved by fans | A second fluid, often cooling water, on the shell side |
| Heat Transfer Surface | Finned tubes that extend the air-side surface | Bare or enhanced tubes inside a closed shell |
| Utilities | Fan power, with no cooling water | A cooling water supply and return, or a process stream |
| Footprint | Large plot area for the tube bundles and fans | Compact shell, plus the cooling water system |

**Q: How does a fin fan cooler work?**
A: A fin fan cooler passes the hot process fluid through rows of finned tubes while fans move ambient air across the outside of the bundle. The fins enlarge the air-side surface, so the air absorbs heat from the tubes and carries it away, and the fluid leaves cooler with no cooling water involved. Forced draft designs mount the fans below the bundle, the fin fan exchanger layout of IPP's Balke Durr units.

**Q: What is an ambient air vaporizer?**
A: Ambient air vaporizers warm a cryogenic liquid with heat drawn from the surrounding air, so the liquid boils into gas inside finned aluminum tubes without fans, steam or electric heaters. IPP's Finnco 20AL/24F carries 24 finned aluminum extrusions rated 34.5 bar (500 psi) for service to -253.9 °C (-425 °F), sized for 20,000 SCFH.

**Q: How are the fans arranged?**
A: Each Balke Durr fin fan air cooler mounts two 2,500 mm (98.4 in) ventilator fans underneath the tube bundle, rated for 46.5 m³/s (98,500 CFM) between them, while each Hiross unit carries an electric fan inside its carbon steel housing. The Finnco vaporizer needs no fan.

**Q: What sizes are available?**
A: Heat transfer areas run from 2.4 m² (25.8 ft²) on each Hiross unit to 2,100 m² (22,850 ft²) on each Balke Durr cooler, whose tubes reach 8,350 mm (328.7 in) in length. A data sheet for either design follows on request.

**Q: What pressure and temperature ratings do the tubes carry?**
A: Balke Durr tubes are rated 1 bar (14.5 psi) at 60 °C (140 °F) working and 3.2 bar (46.4 psi) at 135 °C (275 °F) design, the Hiross tubes 12 bar (174 psi) at 150 °C (302 °F), and the Finnco vaporizer 34.5 bar (500 psi) down to -253.9 °C (-425 °F).

**Q: What are the tubes and fins made of?**
