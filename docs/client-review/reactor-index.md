## Batch-Type Agitated vs Body Only Reactors

Understanding the difference between these two reactor subtypes is the key purchase decision when buying used reactors from IPP. Here is how they compare across key factors.

| Factor | Batch-Type Agitated | Batch Type Body Only |
|-----|-----|-----|
| What Is Included | Vessel, jacket, heads, nozzles, agitation drive, impeller, shaft seal, baffles | Vessel, jacket, heads, nozzles only — no agitation |
| Units in Stock | Larger inventory position | Smaller, specialized inventory |
| Materials Available | Glass-lined, SS, Hastelloy, Titanium, Alloy 20, Monel, Inconel | Glass-lined (all units) |
| Manufacturers | Pfaudler, DeDietrich, GPS, UGE, and 150+ others | Pfaudler, DeDietrich, UGE, EHW Thale |
| Capacity Range | 2 L to 80,000 L (0.5 to 21,150 gal) | 94.6 L to 3,800 L (25 to 1,000 gal) |
| Best For | Complete turnkey reactor installations requiring mixing | Replacement vessel shells or when buyer has existing drives and agitators |
| Agitation Included | Yes — drive, impeller, seal, baffles | No — vessel shell only |
| Conditions Available | Used, re-glassed, new, unused, refurbished | Re-glassed, used, new, needs reglass, unused |

## Frequently Asked Questions About Buying Used Reactors

Answers to the most common questions from procurement and project teams evaluating used batch reactors from IPP.

**Q: What types of reactors does IPP stock?**
A: IPP organizes its reactor inventory across seven subtypes: batch-type agitated reactors, batch type body only reactors, fixed bed, fluid bed, hydrogenation, polymerization, and tubular. Agitated reactors are complete systems with drives, impellers, and seals; body only reactors are reactor shells without agitation components. The remaining subtypes are specialty reactor configurations — fixed bed and fluid bed for catalytic gas-phase reactions, hydrogenation for high-pressure hydrogen reactions, polymerization for polymer processes, and tubular for continuous-flow chemistry. Browse by subtype: [Batch-Type Agitated](batch-type-agitated/) | [Batch Type Body Only](batch-type-body-only/) | [Fixed Bed](fixed-bed/) | [Fluid Bed](fluid-bed/) | [Hydrogenation](hydrogenation/) | [Polymerization](polymerization/) | [Tubular](tubular/)

**Q: How much can I save purchasing used reactors versus new?**
A: Purchasing used reactors from IPP can save up to 50% of capital and 90% of lead time versus buying new. The exact savings depend on manufacturer, capacity, material of construction, and condition. IPP provides detailed quotes with full specification matching on request. Contact IPP for pricing on specific units from the current reactor inventory.

**Q: Does IPP sell new reactors as well as used?**

**Q: Can IPP refurbish or re-glass a reactor?**

**Q: What is the difference between agitated and body only reactors?**
A: Batch-type agitated reactors are complete systems that include the reactor vessel, agitation drive, impeller, shaft seal, and baffles. Batch type body only reactors are reactor shells — vessel, jacket, heads, and nozzles — sold without agitation components. Body only reactors are suited for buyers who already have drives and agitators or need replacement vessels for existing installations. IPP stocks agitated reactors and body only reactors.

**Q: What manufacturers make batch-type agitated reactors?**
A: IPP stocks batch-type agitated reactors from 150+ manufacturers. The largest inventory positions are Pfaudler and DeDietrich, with additional manufacturers including Gale Process Solutions, UGE, Mechanicca Sarda, B. Braun + Diessel Biotech, Schwelm, and many others. [Browse all agitated reactors](batch-type-agitated/)

**Q: What manufacturers make body only reactors?**
A: Body only reactors are available from a smaller set of specialized manufacturers. Pfaudler, DeDietrich, and UGE are the primary manufacturers, with EHW Thale, Schwelm, and others also represented. [Browse all body only reactors](batch-type-body-only/)

**Q: Can I buy a body only reactor and add my own agitation?**
A: Body only reactors are sold as reactor shells with vessel, jacket, heads, and nozzles. The nozzle schedules on body only reactors typically include openings sized for agitator installation. Buyers commonly pair body only shells with their own existing drives or purchase agitation components separately. Contact IPP for details on specific body only reactor nozzle schedules and agitation compatibility.
