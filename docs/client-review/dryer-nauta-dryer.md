## Conical Screw vs Double Cone Dryers

Conical screw and double cone dryers both dry a batch against heated vessel walls, usually under vacuum; what moves, the screw or the whole vessel, decides the difference. These factors separate the two designs.

| Factor | Conical Screw (Nauta) Dryer | Double Cone Dryer |
|-----|-----|-----|
| Vessel | A stationary, vertical, jacketed cone | A double-cone vessel that rotates on a horizontal axis |
| Agitation | A cantilevered screw rotates on its own axis and orbits the cone wall | The whole vessel turns to tumble the batch |
| Internal Parts | Screw, shaft and seals in the product zone | No internal moving parts |
| Batch Fill | Works across a wide partial-batch range, since the screw does the mixing | Needs a set fill level so the product cascades |
| Heating | Jacket, plus a heated screw on some models | Heated vessel walls |
| In IPP's Inventory | Krauss Maffei, Bolz, Vrieco Nauta and other units from 0.1 m³ (3.5 ft³) to 19.9 m³ (704 ft³) | [Listed on IPP's twin shell and double cone dryer page](https://internationalprocessplants.com/process-equipment/dryer/twin-shell-and-double-cone-dryer/) |

**Q: How does a Nauta conical screw dryer work?**
A: A Nauta conical screw dryer combines orbital mixing with indirect heating, usually under vacuum. A free-hanging screw rotates on its own axis to lift the product from the bottom of the cone, while an orbiting arm carries the screw along the cone wall. Steam, hot water or thermal oil in the jacket heats the product through the wall, the vacuum lowers the boiling point of the moisture or solvent, and the vapors leave through a filter at the top of the conical screw vacuum dryer on their way to a condenser.

**Q: Who makes Nauta dryers?**
A: The Nauta name comes from Johannes Ewardus Nauta, who founded Nautamix in Haarlem, the Netherlands, and introduced the original conical screw mixer in 1939. Hosokawa Micron acquired Nautamix and Vrieco and merged them into Hosokawa Micron B.V. in 1987, which builds the Nauta and Vrieco Nauta lines. Krauss Maffei, Bolz and other OEMs build conical screw dryers on the same principle, and IPP lists every Nauta dryer and conical screw dryer together, while the Nauta mixer has its own [Nauta mixer](https://internationalprocessplants.com/process-equipment/mixer/nauta-mixer/) page.
