## Welded Plate vs Gasketed Plate and Frame Heat Exchangers

A welded plate exchanger joins its plates with welds, while a gasketed plate and frame exchanger seals each plate with an elastomer gasket. The rows below contrast sealing, ratings, access and capacity changes.

| Factor | Welded Plate | Gasketed Plate and Frame |
|-----|-----|-----|
| Plate Sealing | Welded plate pack, with no gaskets between the channels | An elastomer gasket on each plate |
| Ratings | Set by the welded pack and frame, up to 16 bar (232 psi) and 250 °C (482 °F) in stock | Limited by the gasket material |
| Access | Bolted side panels open to reach the channels | The plate pack opens when the frame bolts are released |
| Capacity Changes | Fixed plate pack | Plates are added to or removed from the frame |
| In IPP's Inventory | Alfa Laval Compabloc and Kapp units | [Listed on IPP's plate and frame heat exchanger page](/process-equipment/heat-exchanger/plate-and-frame/) |

A: A welded plate heat exchanger stacks corrugated plates welded together into a pack, so the two fluids run through alternating channels and exchange heat across each plate. In a Compabloc the channels run cross-flow, and baffles turn the fluids back and forth through the pack, which delivers counter-current heat transfer in a compact welded heat exchanger. A frame of girders, top and bottom heads and four side panels holds the pack.

**Q: What is an Alfa Laval Compabloc?**
A: Compabloc is Alfa Laval's family of welded plate heat exchangers: a fully welded plate pack inside a bolted frame whose side panels open for access to the channels. The Compabloc heat exchanger units in IPP's stock come from the CP30, CPL30, CPL20, CPF, CPK40 and CPX series, and Alfa Laval Compabloc models carry their plate count in the type code, such as 60, 80 or 160 plates.

**Q: Does a welded plate heat exchanger use gaskets?**
A: No, not between the plates. The plates are welded into a fully welded heat exchanger pack, so no gasket separates the two fluids, while the frame's removable side panels seal against the pack with panel gaskets.
