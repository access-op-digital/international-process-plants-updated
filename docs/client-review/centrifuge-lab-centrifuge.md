## Continuous-Flow vs Batch Lab Centrifuges

Continuous-flow and batch lab centrifuges both use centrifugal force to separate components by density, and they differ in how material enters and leaves the machine. Here is how the two designs compare across key factors.

| Factor | Continuous-Flow Centrifuge | Batch Centrifuge |
|-----|-----|-----|
| Operation | Liquid is pumped into the spinning bowl and the clarified liquid flows out without stopping | Samples are loaded, spun and unloaded after the rotor stops |
| Volume | Large volumes without repeated loading and unloading | A fixed volume per run |
| Solids | Held in the bowl until the run ends or the solids space is full | Collected in the tube, beaker or basket after each run |
| Fluid Path | Rotating seals keep the fluid path closed during the run | Loaded and unloaded by hand between runs |
| In IPP's Inventory | Carr Centritech Centritec Cell 8 units and the Sharples Type 1P | A carbon steel beaker centrifuge |

**Q: How does a continuous flow centrifuge work?**
A: A continuous flow centrifuge pumps the liquid through a sealed inlet into the spinning bowl, where centrifugal force drives the denser solids to the bowl wall while the clarified liquid flows out through a separate outlet. The unit runs until the batch is processed or the solids space is full, then stops for the solids to be harvested, and rotating seals keep the fluid path closed during the run.

**Q: What is the Sharples Type 1P super centrifuge?**
A: Sharples built the Type 1P as a high-speed tubular bowl laboratory super centrifuge for continuous separation of immiscible liquids and clarification of solids from liquids. Liquid enters at the base of the vertical bowl, the separated liquid phases leave through spouts at the top, and the solids stay in the bowl for cleaning after the run. IPP's unit has a pressure-tight bowl and an air or steam turbine drive rated to 50,000 rpm.

**Q: What is the Carr Centritech Centritec Cell 8?**
A: IMS lists the Centritec Cell 8 as a single-use insert flow centrifuge from Carr Centritech, with a precise servo drive, heating and refrigeration, a 2 to 150 °C (36 to 302 °F) temperature control range, programmable HMI controls and data export. Its capacity is 6 to 120 L/hr, and IPP stocks CC 46, CC 47 and CC 48 units in stainless steel 316L and 316.
