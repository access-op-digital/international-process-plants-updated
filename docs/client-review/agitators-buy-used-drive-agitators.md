## Drive Agitator vs Glass-Lined Drive vs Shaft/Mixer

Buying the correct agitator type depends on your vessel construction and process requirements. This comparison shows when each type applies.

| Attribute | Drive Agitator | Glass-Lined Drive | Shaft/Mixer |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| Vessel Type | Open tanks, SS reactors, CS vessels | Glass-lined reactors only | Any vessel (shaft replacement) |
| Construction | Carbon steel, SS 316, Hastelloy | Borosilicate glass-coated wetted parts | Hastelloy C-276, titanium, SS |
| Power Range | 0.22–100.7 kW (0.3–135 HP) | 0.75–87.2 kW (1–117 HP) | N/A (shaft only, no motor) |
| Primary Manufacturers | Lightnin, Philadelphia, Chemineer | Pfaudler, DeDietrich | Lightnin |
| Mounting | Bridge, right-angle, clamp-on, flange | Flange-mounted (closed tank) | Couples to drive unit |
| Corrosion Protection | Metallurgy-dependent (alloy selection) | Glass lining prevents metal contamination | Exotic alloy resistance |
| Best For | General chemical mixing, open tanks | Pharma/chemical glass-lined reactors | Exotic alloy shaft replacement |

## Frequently Asked Questions About Buying Used Drive Agitators

Answers to common questions from process teams and procurement specialists evaluating used drive agitators.

**Q: What is a drive agitator?**
A: A drive agitator is the motor, gearbox, and coupling assembly that powers agitator shafts in reactor and tank applications. Drive agitators mount to vessels via right-angle, bridge-mount, clamp-on, or flange-mount configurations and deliver rotational power through gear reduction to the mixing shaft below.

**Q: What is the difference between right-angle and bridge-mount agitator drives?**
A: Right-angle drives mount on the vessel nozzle or flange and transmit power through a 90-degree gearbox to the vertical shaft. They suit closed vessels with top nozzle access. Bridge-mount drives sit on a structural beam spanning the vessel opening, supporting the motor independently of the vessel shell. Bridge-mount configurations handle higher HP loads and serve large open-top tanks where the vessel wall cannot support the drive weight.

**Q: What is the difference between a drive agitator and a glass-lined drive agitator?**
A: Drive agitators use bare metal construction (carbon steel, stainless steel, or Hastelloy) and serve open tanks and non-glass-lined vessels where corrosion resistance comes from the shaft and impeller metallurgy. Glass-lined drive agitators feature borosilicate glass-coated wetted parts specifically designed for glass-lined reactors where metal contamination must be prevented. If your vessel is glass-lined, you need a [glass-lined drive](/equipment/agitator/glass-lined/). If it is not, a standard drive agitator is the correct choice.

**Q: What gear ratios are available in IPP's drive agitator inventory?**
A: IPP's drive agitator inventory covers gear ratios from 2.5:1 to 70:1. Lower ratios (2.5:1 to 10:1) deliver higher output shaft speeds for low-viscosity blending. Higher ratios (25:1 to 70:1) produce lower output speeds with greater torque for high-viscosity suspension and heavy-duty chemical processing.

**Q: What materials of construction are available?**
A: IPP stocks drive agitators in carbon steel, stainless steel 316, stainless steel austenitic, stainless steel 316Ti, and Hastelloy construction. Contact IPP to discuss material requirements for your application.

**Q: Are VFD-ready drive agitators available?**
A: Yes. IPP stocks several VFD-ready (Variable Frequency Drive) agitators that accept variable-speed motor controllers for adjustable output shaft speed. VFD-ready drives allow you to tune the mixing intensity to your specific process without changing the mechanical gearbox.

**Q: How much can I save buying used drive agitators versus new?**
A: Buying used drive agitators from IPP can save up to 50% of capital and 90% of lead time versus buying new. The exact savings depend on manufacturer, power rating, material of construction, and condition. Contact IPP for pricing on specific units.

**Q: What documentation comes with a used drive agitator from IPP?**
A: Every drive agitator from IPP ships with a complete mechanical data package including power rating, gear ratio, input/output RPM, transmission type (direct-coupled or belt-drive), shaft dimensions, mounting configuration, and condition report. Material test reports (MTRs) are included where available. IPP provides dimensional drawings for integration into your existing reactor or vessel installation.

**Q: Does IPP handle shipping and freight?**
