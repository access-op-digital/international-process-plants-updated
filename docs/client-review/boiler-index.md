## Frequently Asked Questions About Buying Used Boilers

Answers to the most common questions from process teams and procurement teams evaluating used industrial boilers.

**Q: What types of boilers does IPP stock?**
A: IPP stocks 34 boilers across three subtypes: steam boilers (23 units), hot oil boilers (10 units), and miscellaneous boilers (1 unit). Designs include firetube and water tube configurations. Boiler types include fire-tube, water-tube, hot oil heaters, waste heat recovery boilers, and electric boilers. Browse the IMS inventory for current availability, or [contact IPP](https://internationalprocessplants.com/contact/) to discuss your specific requirements.

**Q: What is the difference between steam and hot oil boilers?**
A: Steam boilers generate steam or hot water by heating water through combustion or electric elements—used for process heating, power generation, sanitation, and HVAC support. Hot oil boilers heat thermal oil for high-temperature, closed-loop systems and deliver process temperatures above the practical limits of steam without the high pressures required for equivalent steam operation. Hot oil boilers in IPP's inventory operate at temperatures up to 350°C (662°F).

**Q: What capacity and pressure ranges are available?**
A: Design capacities in IPP's boiler inventory range from 46.5 kg/h (102.5 lb/h) for small electric steam units up to 40,350 kg/h (89,000 lb/h) for large industrial water tube steam boilers. Power outputs span from 7.5 kW (10 HP) to 13,100 kW (17,550 HP), and pressure ratings from 1.03 bar (15 psi) to 91 bar (1,300 psi). Contact IPP to match capacity and pressure to your process load.

**Q: What fuel types are supported?**
A: Fuel types in current inventory include natural gas, diesel, heavy fuel oil, gas oil, and electric. Multi-fuel boilers (natural gas plus diesel, or natural gas plus heavy fuel oil) are also available. Burner manufacturers include Cleaver Brooks, Riello, Saacke, Hamworthy Combustion, Bosch, Babcock Wanson, and others.

**Q: How much can I save buying a used boiler versus new?**
A: Buying used boilers from IPP can save up to 50% of capital and 90% of lead time versus buying new. Exact savings depend on boiler type, manufacturer, capacity, pressure rating, fuel system, and condition. Used boilers also avoid the long fabrication and ASME stamp lead times typical of new orders. Contact IPP for pricing on specific units in current inventory.

**Q: What condition grades are available for used boilers?**
A: IPP stocks boilers in two condition grades: good used (32 units) and unused surplus (2 units—a Euroboilers TH/AR-V1500 hot oil boiler and a Bosch UL-S firetube steam boiler). Unused boilers were manufactured but never installed in process service, offering new-equipment performance with immediate availability. Contact IPP for condition details on specific boilers of interest.
