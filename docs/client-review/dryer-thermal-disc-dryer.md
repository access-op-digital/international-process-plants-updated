## Disc Dryers vs Paddle Dryers

Disc and paddle dryers both heat the product indirectly through a hollow rotating shaft and a trough, and the shape of the heated elements on that shaft tells them apart. Below, a disc dryer meets a paddle dryer on five factors.

| Factor | Disc Dryer | Paddle Dryer |
|-----|-----|-----|
| Heated Elements | Hollow, double-walled discs along a central shaft | Interleaved wedge- or fan-shaped hollow paddles on one or more shafts |
| Agitation | Disc rotation with peripheral mixing elements moves the product forward | Angled paddles knead, shear and convey the product |
| Running Resistance | Lower resistance through the product bed | Higher torque in dense, viscous phases |
| Heat Transfer Area | Large surface area packed into a short footprint | Heated paddles add surface relative to the working volume |
| In IPP's Inventory | Bepex, Bethlehem and Strong Scott units | [Porcupine paddle processors, listed on IPP's porcupine dryer page](https://internationalprocessplants.com/process-equipment/dryer/porcupine-dryer/) |

**Q: How does a torus disc dryer work?**
A: A torus disc dryer heats the product indirectly: steam or thermal oil flows through a hollow central shaft and the double-walled discs mounted on it, and jacketed vessels add heat transfer surface through the walls. The slowly turning discs mix the product and move it from the inlet toward the discharge, and the vapor leaves through an upper vapor dome. Bepex sells the design as the TorusDisc.

**Q: Can a thermal disc dryer cool a product as well?**
A: Yes. The same machine heats, dries or cools, depending on the medium circulated through the rotor discs and any vessel jacket, from steam or thermal oil to chilled water or glycol. IMS lists IPP's Bepex TD48-9 units as torus disc coolers, with rotors rated 6.9 bar (100 psi).

**Q: How is residence time controlled?**
A: An adjustable overflow weir at the discharge end sets how long the product stays in the trough, and the Bepex Model 60-14 in IPP's inventory carries an adjustable product discharge weir for that purpose. The Bethlehem processor discharges through a 305 mm (12 in) screw instead.
