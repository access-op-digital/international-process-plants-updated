## Flash Dryers vs Spray Dryers

Flash dryers and spray dryers both dry a product in contact with hot air; the feed each one accepts and the way it meets the air divide the two. Each row below sets a flash dryer against a spray dryer.

| Factor | Flash Dryer | Spray Dryer |
|-----|-----|-----|
| Feed | Wet solids, powders, crystals and filter cakes; spin flash designs also take pastes and sludges | Pumpable liquids, solutions, slurries and emulsions |
| Feed Entry | A screw feeder, venturi or rotary valve meters the wet feed into the hot air stream | A nozzle or rotary disc atomizer sprays the liquid as a fine mist into the drying chamber |
| Drying | Surface moisture flashes off within seconds while the air conveys the solids | Droplets dry in contact with hot air inside a large drying chamber |
| Particle Size | Dries the existing particles, with limited control of final size; a classifier sets the fineness on some units | Forms spherical, uniform powders with controlled particle size and bulk density |
| Footprint | A compact duct or column with a lower capital cost | A large drying tower and atomization hardware |
| In IPP's Inventory | APV Anhydro, Aljet, Hosokawa Alpine and AKSH Engineering units | [Listed on IPP's spray dryer page](https://internationalprocessplants.com/process-equipment/dryer/spray-dryer/) |

**Q: How does an industrial flash dryer work?**
A: An industrial flash dryer suspends wet particles in a high-velocity stream of hot air, which evaporates their surface moisture within seconds while carrying them through the drying duct. A screw feeder, venturi or rotary valve meters the wet feed into air heated by a gas burner or air heater, the particles dry as they travel up the column, and a cyclone or bag filter separates the dry powder from the exhaust air. Because the moisture evaporates so fast, the particles stay near the wet-bulb temperature of the air through the flash drying process.

**Q: How does a spin flash dryer work?**
A: A spin flash dryer adds a high-speed rotor at the base of the drying chamber, which breaks up wet cakes, pastes and sludges as hot air enters in a swirling, tangential pattern. The air lifts the fine, dried particles up the chamber to a cyclone or bag filter, while heavier lumps fall back to the rotor for more disintegration, and the same design is also sold as a rotary flash dryer. IPP's APV Anhydro III Number 55 is a spin flash dryer system with its air heater and filters.

**Q: What does the classifier on a flash dryer do?**
A: A classifier keeps particles in the dryer until they reach the target particle size and then releases them. On the Hosokawa Alpine MDV-4, the classifier rotor turns at 45 to 450 rpm, and its speed and the air flow set the product fineness, 100 to 325 mesh in the unit's original design. The Aljet SBDF-50 carries an integral internal classifier.
