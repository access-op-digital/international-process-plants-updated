Industrial chillers remove heat from liquids or gases through refrigeration cycles or heat exchange systems, ensuring equipment and processes operate within required temperature ranges. Refrigerants across the inventory include R-22, R-134a, R-410a, R-404a, R-123, and NH3 (ammonia). Materials of construction include carbon steel and galvanized. Condition options include good used, unused surplus, and new.

In chemical processing, chillers maintain reaction temperatures, cool reactors, and stabilize sensitive formulations. In food and beverage production, chillers cool ingredients, packaging lines, and production environments. In plastics processing, chillers remove heat from molds, extruders, and injection molding machines. ## Frequently Asked Questions About Buying Used Chillers

Answers to the most common questions from process teams and procurement teams evaluating used industrial chillers.

**Q: What types of chillers does IPP stock?**
A: IPP stocks 36 industrial chillers across 6 subtypes: compressor chillers (14 units), water cooled chillers (8 units), low temp chillers (5 units), air cooled chillers (5 units), tower chillers (3 units), and ice machine chillers (1 unit). Browse the IMS inventory for current availability, or [contact IPP](https://internationalprocessplants.com/contact/) to discuss your specific cooling requirements.

**Q: What is the difference between air cooled and water cooled chillers?**
A: Air cooled chillers reject heat directly to the atmosphere through finned-tube condensers and condenser fans, making them suitable for facilities without cooling water availability. Water cooled chillers reject heat to a separate cooling water loop, typically through a cooling tower, and generally achieve higher efficiency and larger capacities. In IPP's inventory, water cooled chillers reach 3,000 refrigeration tons (10,550 kW), while air cooled chillers reach 113 refrigeration tons (397 kW).

**Q: What refrigerants are used in IPP's chiller inventory?**
A: Refrigerants across IPP's chiller inventory include R-22, R-134a, R-410a, R-404a, R-123, and NH3 (ammonia). Older units may use HCFC refrigerants such as R-22; HFC refrigerants such as R-134a and R-410a are common in more recent units; ammonia is used in industrial refrigeration applications. Note that R-22 has been phased out of new equipment production globally and may require refrigerant replacement or retrofit for long-term operation.

**Q: Who are the leading chiller manufacturers in IPP's inventory?**
A: Top chiller manufacturers in IPP's inventory include Trane (7 units), York and York International (7 units combined), Bitzer (3 units), APV (2 units), Marley (2 units), and SCM Frigo (2 units). **Q: What capacity range is available?**
