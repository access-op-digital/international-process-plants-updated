## Variable Intensity Mixers vs Rotary Batch Mixers

Munson builds both designs, and a turning shaft inside a fixed vessel versus a turning drum with fixed flights separates them.

| Factor | Variable Intensity Mixer | Rotary Batch Mixer |
|-----|-----|-----|
| Vessel | A stationary horizontal cylinder | A horizontal drum turning on external trunnion rings |
| Mixing Element | One high-speed agitator shaft, 120 to 600 rpm on standard models | Internal flights fixed to the turning drum, with no internal shaft or seals |
| Operation | Continuous flow through the vessel | Batches loaded and discharged through stationary inlets and outlets |
| Mixing Action | Low to high shear, set by agitator speed | A four-way tumble, turn, cut and fold |
| Liquids | Injection ports along the vessel | Spray lines over the moving bed |
