## High-Pressure vs High Shear Homogenizers

A high-pressure homogenizer forces the product through a narrow valve gap, while a high shear homogenizer works it between a rotor and a stator. Mechanism, pressure, droplet size and flow separate the two in the rows below.

| Factor | High-Pressure | High Shear |
|-----|-----|-----|
| Mechanism | A piston pump pushes the product through an adjustable homogenizing valve | A fast rotor turns inside a fixed stator, shearing the product in the gap |
| Operating Pressure | Hundreds of bar, up to 1,200 bar (17,400 psi) in stock | Low, up to 10 bar (145 psi) in stock |
| Droplet Size | Very fine, uniform droplets and particles | Coarser dispersions and emulsions |
| Flow Rates in Stock | 160 L/h (0.7 GPM) to 20,000 L/h (88.1 GPM) | 10,000 L/h (44 GPM) to 20,000 L/h (88.1 GPM) |

**Q: How does a high pressure homogenizer work?**
A: A high pressure homogenizer uses a piston pump to force the product through a narrow, adjustable gap in a homogenizing valve. The sudden pressure drop, turbulence and shear in the gap break droplets and particles into smaller, uniform sizes, and a second stage can follow to separate clusters. High pressure homogenizers in IPP's stock reach 1,200 bar (17,400 psi).

**Q: What is a rotor-stator homogenizer?**
A: Rotor-stator machines spin a rotor at high speed inside a slotted stator, so the product is drawn in, sheared in the narrow gap and thrown out through the stator openings. This high shear homogenizer action disperses and emulsifies the product in continuous flow, and the dispersing homogenizer units in stock are a BWS Technologies inline machine and a Hoelschertechnik Gorator.

**Q: What do single, two and three-stage homogenizing valves mean?**
A: Each stage is a homogenizing valve: a single-stage machine has one, while a two-stage machine adds a second valve at lower pressure behind the main one. The APV Gaulin 5283MC carries a two-stage Dyna-jet valve with a hydraulic actuator, and the SPX BT16-66VH lists three stages.

**Q: What flow rates and pressures are available?**
A: Flow rates run from 160 L/h (0.7 GPM) on the SPX BT16-66VH to 20,000 L/h (88.1 GPM) on the APV Gaulin and BWS units, and pressures from 4 bar (58 psi) on the Gorator to 1,200 bar (17,400 psi) on the SPX. Send your target flow and pressure and IPP checks each machine against them.
