**Who:** Scott Schwartz
**Quote:** "Instead of saying used, we say good used. Surplus. You can have it good used, you can have it re-glassed, you can have it new. That'll really help differentiate us versus our competition."

### 3. "Glass-Lined" NOT "Glass" — Critical Distinction
