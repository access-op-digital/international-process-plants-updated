Inside the bowl, the scroll conveyor scrapes the settled solids from the bowl wall and pushes them up the conical beach to the solids discharge ports. It turns at a slightly different speed from the bowl, and that differential speed sets how long the solids stay in the machine. IPP's decanter scroll stock includes Sharples P3400, Alfa Laval NX314 and Dorr Oliver conveyors.
A rotating assembly combines the bowl and the scroll conveyor fitted inside it, often with the gearbox on the end. The main motor drives the bowl at high speed while the gearbox sets the scroll's differential speed. IPP's rotating assemblies are a Sharples P-2000 with bowl, screw conveyor and one gearbox, and a Sangerhausen SKZSNh450S with a 450 mm (17.7 in) by 900 mm (35.4 in) bowl, supplied without a gearbox.
The gearbox, or backdrive, on a decanter centrifuge controls the speed difference between the fast-spinning bowl and the scroll conveyor, and adjusting it changes how quickly the solids move to discharge. IPP's inventory includes a Bird decanter gearbox with 10 teeth on the spline, and the Sharples P-2000 rotating assembly comes with one gearbox.
The parts fit the Sharples P3400 and P-2000, the Alfa Laval NX314, the Dorr Oliver 16L and Flottweg Z3L, the Sangerhausen SKZSNh450S and a Bird decanter. For Alfa Laval decanter parts, the listing is the NX314 scroll conveyor with two plows.
IMS lists stainless steel 316 on the Dorr Oliver, Sharples P3400, Sharples P-2000 and Sangerhausen parts, and other stainless steel on the Alfa Laval NX314 conveyor. The remaining listings state their material where IMS records it.
