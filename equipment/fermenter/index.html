Materials of construction include stainless steel 304, 316, and 316L for sanitary pharmaceutical and food-grade fermentation duties... ➔

Materials of construction include stainless steel 304, 316, and 316L, with one additional austenitic grade. 316L stainless steel is preferred for sanitary pharmaceutical and food-grade operations, while SS 304 and SS 316 cover broader industrial and biotech duties. Condition options include good used (21 units) and unused surplus (3 units).

Fermenters are designed for precise control of temperature, agitation, pH, and dissolved oxygen, which is key to consistent batch quality and regulatory compliance. Key features across the inventory include CIP capability, dimple or standard jackets, sidewall baffles, mechanical or clamp-on drives, and impeller types including flat-blade turbine, paddle, and Rushton. Buying used fermenters from IPP can save up to 50% of capital and 90% of lead time versus buying new. ⬅
