Comparing Used Pulverizer Types

Each pulverizer subtype targets different particle sizes, throughputs, and feed materials. Below is a comparison of the four pulverizer subtypes IPP stocks in current inventory.

Pulverizer Type Size-Reduction Mechanism Typical Fineness Best For
Granulator / Disintegrator (55 units) Rotating knives, pins, or impact rotor Coarse to medium granules Pharmaceutical granulations, food, plastics, chemicals
Jet (24 units) High-velocity compressed gas (air/steam) particle-on-particle impact Ultra-fine, micronized (sub-micron to ~10 µm) Heat-sensitive APIs, pigments, fine ceramics, contamination-sensitive feeds
Hammer (18 units) Rotating hammers and screen-controlled impact grinding Coarse to medium powders Minerals, mining, recycling, chemicals, high-throughput duties
Comminutor (2 units) Rotating impeller with screen for controlled particle sizing Medium powders, controlled distribution Pharmaceutical and chemical sizing, lump breaking
