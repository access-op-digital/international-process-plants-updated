Attribute Drive Agitator Glass-Lined Drive Shaft/Mixer
Vessel Type Open tanks, SS reactors, CS vessels Glass-lined reactors only Any vessel (shaft replacement)
Construction Carbon steel, SS 316, Hastelloy Borosilicate glass-coated wetted parts Hastelloy C-276, titanium, SS
Power Range 0.22–100.7 kW (0.3–135 HP) 0.75–87.2 kW (1–117 HP) N/A (shaft only, no motor)
Primary Manufacturers Lightnin, Philadelphia, Chemineer Pfaudler, DeDietrich Lightnin
Mounting Bridge, right-angle, clamp-on, flange Flange-mounted (closed tank) Couples to drive unit
Corrosion Protection Metallurgy-dependent (alloy selection) Glass lining prevents metal contamination Exotic alloy resistance
Best For General chemical mixing, open tanks Pharma/chemical glass-lined reactors Exotic alloy shaft replacement

Frequently Asked Questions About Buying Used Drive Agitators

Answers to common questions from process teams and procurement specialists evaluating used drive agitators.

A drive agitator is the motor, gearbox, and coupling assembly that powers agitator shafts in reactor and tank applications. Drive agitators mount to vessels via right-angle, bridge-mount, clamp-on, or flange-mount configurations and deliver rotational power through gear reduction to the mixing shaft below.
Right-angle drives mount on the vessel nozzle or flange and transmit power through a 90-degree gearbox to the vertical shaft. They suit closed vessels with top nozzle access. Bridge-mount drives sit on a structural beam spanning the vessel opening, supporting the motor independently of the vessel shell. Bridge-mount configurations handle higher HP loads and serve large open-top tanks where the vessel wall cannot support the drive weight.
Drive agitators use bare metal construction (carbon steel, stainless steel, or Hastelloy) and serve open tanks and non-glass-lined vessels where corrosion resistance comes from the shaft and impeller metallurgy. Glass-lined drive agitators feature borosilicate glass-coated wetted parts specifically designed for glass-lined reactors where metal contamination must be prevented. If your vessel is glass-lined, you need a glass-lined drive. If it is not, a standard drive agitator is the correct choice.
IPP's drive agitator inventory covers gear ratios from 2.5:1 to 70:1. Lower ratios (2.5:1 to 10:1) deliver higher output shaft speeds for low-viscosity blending. Higher ratios (25:1 to 70:1) produce lower output speeds with greater torque for high-viscosity suspension and heavy-duty chemical processing.
IPP stocks drive agitators in carbon steel, stainless steel 316, stainless steel austenitic, stainless steel 316Ti, and Hastelloy construction. Contact IPP to discuss material requirements for your application.
Yes. IPP stocks several VFD-ready (Variable Frequency Drive) agitators that accept variable-speed motor controllers for adjustable output shaft speed. VFD-ready drives allow you to tune the mixing intensity to your specific process without changing the mechanical gearbox.
