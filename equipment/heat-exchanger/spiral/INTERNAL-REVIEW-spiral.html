Buy Used Spiral Heat Exchangers for Sale

Buying good used and new spiral heat exchangers from International Process Plants (IPP) advances counter-current heat transfer, single-channel flow paths, and compact spiral construction while overcoming issues like long exchanger fabrication times and new-unit budgets. Chemical, pharmaceutical, food and specialty manufacturers weigh units here from Alfa Laval, Kuhni, American Heat Reclaiming, GAB Neumann, GEA Canzler and other OEM manufacturers. Exchanger surfaces cover 0.4 m² (4.3 ft²) to 205.8 m² (2,200 ft²), in stainless steel, high alloys, glass and graphite. Buying used saves up to 50% of capital and 90% of lead time versus buying new, and IPP has supplied process equipment worldwide since 1980. ▶

Two long metal plates wound around a central core form two concentric channels, one fluid in each, so the streams run counter-current through the coil of a spiral plate exchanger. Alfa Laval spiral plate units in stainless steel, duplex and Hastelloy share the stock with Kuhni coil exchangers, QVF and Schott glass spiral tube units and GAB Neumann graphite exchangers, and every used spiral heat exchanger listed here is in stock.

IPP's IMS inventory system summarizes each unit's heat transfer area, channel or coil ratings, materials and condition grade. New custom fabricated stainless steel equipment is available through IPP's Gale Process Solutions (GPS) subsidiary with 12 to 16 week average delivery. ▲

Spiral Plate vs Spiral Tube Heat Exchangers

A spiral plate exchanger winds two plates into a pair of concentric channels, while a spiral tube exchanger coils one tube inside a shell or tank. Below, construction, flow pattern, surface and materials set the plate and tube styles apart.

Factor Spiral Plate Spiral Tube
Construction Two plates wound around a core into two rectangular channels One or more tubes coiled inside a shell or tank
Flow Pattern Single channel per fluid, counter-current along the spiral Tube-side fluid in the coil, shell-side fluid around it
Heat Transfer Area Large areas in a compact body, up to 205.8 m² (2,200 ft²) in stock Smaller areas, from 0.4 m² (4.3 ft²) on glass coils
Materials in Stock Stainless steel, duplex, Hastelloy, Alloy 20, carbon steel Stainless steel 316L and glass
