Buy Used Graphite Block Heat Exchangers for Sale

Buying good used, refurbished, and unused graphite block heat exchangers from International Process Plants (IPP) extends corrosion-resistant heat transfer, compact block construction, and cross-flow channel design while dodging issues like long graphite machining queues and new-block pricing. Chemical, pharmaceutical, food and specialty manufacturers compare blocks here from Ralph Coidan, Carbone Lorraine, Vicarb, GAB Neumann, SGL Carbon and other OEM manufacturers. Block surfaces span 0.8 m² (8.6 ft²) to 72.7 m² (782 ft²), with process ratings up to 10.3 bar (150 psi). Buying used saves up to 50% of capital and 90% of lead time versus buying new, and IPP has supplied process equipment worldwide since 1980. ▶

Impervious graphite blocks, drilled with two sets of channels that cross without meeting, carry the process and service fluids through one solid body, the design behind every graphite heat exchanger of this type. Ralph Coidan blocks, Carbone Lorraine Polybloc Graphilor units and Vicarb EC exchangers share the stock with GAB Neumann and SGL Carbon blocks, Karbate graphite tube units and an unused Chart brazed aluminum exchanger, and every used block heat exchanger listed here ships from stock.

IPP's IMS inventory system holds each block's heat transfer area, process and service ratings, passes and condition grade. New custom fabricated stainless steel equipment is available through IPP's Gale Process Solutions (GPS) subsidiary with 12 to 16 week average delivery. ▲

Graphite Block vs Shell and Tube Heat Exchangers

A graphite block exchanger routes both fluids through drilled channels in solid graphite, while a shell and tube exchanger carries one fluid inside a tube bundle and the other around it. Construction, flow paths, wetted material and modularity separate them in the table below.

Factor Graphite Block Shell and Tube
Construction Impervious graphite blocks with drilled cross-flow channels, held in a steel shell or between end plates A tube bundle with tubesheets inside a cylindrical shell
Flow Paths Two sets of channels cross inside each block without meeting One fluid in the tubes, the other on the shell side across baffles
Wetted Material Resin-impregnated graphite Carbon steel, stainless steel, alloys or graphite tubes
Modularity Stacked blocks set the heat transfer area Tube count and length set the heat transfer area

A graphite block heat exchanger passes two fluids through separate sets of holes drilled in blocks of impervious, resin-impregnated graphite.
