Buy Used Welded Plate Heat Exchangers for Sale

Buying good used and unused welded plate heat exchangers from International Process Plants (IPP) ensures gasket-free plate heat transfer, full-vacuum duty, and compact block construction while beating issues like long Compabloc lead times and full list prices. Chemical, pharmaceutical, food and specialty manufacturers assess Alfa Laval Compabloc and Kapp units here. Heat transfer areas run from 2.1 m² (22.6 ft²) to 319.5 m² (3,450 ft²), with plates in stainless steel 316L and 316 or Hastelloy C22, C276 and C2000. Buying used saves up to 50% of capital and 90% of lead time versus buying new, and IPP has supplied process equipment worldwide since 1980. ▶

Corrugated plates welded into a stacked pack form alternating channels for the two fluids, and a bolted frame of girders and side panels holds the pack, the design Alfa Laval sells as the Compabloc. CP30, CPL30, CPK40 and CPX units in stainless steel 316L and Hastelloy share the stock with Kapp welded plate exchangers, and each used welded plate heat exchanger listed here leaves from stock.

IPP's IMS inventory system sets out each unit's heat transfer area, plate material, design pressure and temperature, and condition grade. New custom fabricated stainless steel equipment is available through IPP's Gale Process Solutions (GPS) subsidiary with 12 to 16 week average delivery. ▲

Welded Plate vs Gasketed Plate and Frame Heat Exchangers

A welded plate exchanger joins its plates with welds, while a gasketed plate and frame exchanger seals each plate with an elastomer gasket. The rows below contrast sealing, ratings, access and capacity changes.

Factor Welded Plate Gasketed Plate and Frame
Plate Sealing Welded plate pack, with no gaskets between the channels An elastomer gasket on each plate
Ratings Set by the welded pack and frame, up to 16 bar (232 psi) and 250 °C (482 °F) in stock Limited by the gasket material
Access Bolted side panels open to reach the channels The plate pack opens when the frame bolts are released
Capacity Changes Fixed plate pack Plates are added to or removed from the frame

A welded plate heat exchanger stacks corrugated plates welded together into a pack, so the two fluids run through alternating channels and exchange heat across each plate. In a Compabloc the channels run cross-flow, and baffles turn the fluids back and forth through the pack, which delivers counter-current heat transfer in a compact welded heat exchanger. A frame of girders, top and bottom heads and four side panels holds the pack.
Compabloc is Alfa Laval's family of welded plate heat exchangers: a fully welded plate pack inside a bolted frame whose side panels open for access to the channels. The Compabloc heat exchanger units in IPP's stock come from the CP30, CPL30, CPL20, CPF, CPK40 and CPX series, and Alfa Laval Compabloc models carry their plate count in the type code, such as 60, 80 or 160 plates.
No, not between the plates. The plates are welded into a fully welded heat exchanger pack, so no gasket separates the two fluids, while the frame's removable side panels seal against the pack with panel gaskets.
Heat transfer areas run from 2.1 m² (22.6 ft²) on a Compabloc CPF15 to 319.5 m² (3,450 ft²) on the unused CPX75-V-500. A data sheet for any unit follows on request.
Compabloc CP30 and CPL30 units carry design pressures of 6 to 8 barg plus full vacuum at -29 to 185 °C (-20 to 365 °F), the CPK40 units 10 bar (145 psi) plus full vacuum, and the unused CPX units up to 16 bar (232 psi), with the CPX75 rated to 250 °C (482 °F).
Stainless steel 316L and 316 form the plates on the CP30, CPK40 and CPX units and the Kapp HX2125, Hastelloy C22 on CP30, CPL30 and CPF units, Hastelloy C276 on two CPL30 units, and Hastelloy C2000 on the Kapp HXEU 37.5.
