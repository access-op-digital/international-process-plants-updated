Shell and tube heat exchangers have an external cylinder (shell) with internal tubes — one fluid flows through the tubes while the other flows around them. They handle high pressures and are rugged. Plate heat exchangers consist of a series of alloy plates held together by a frame, offering better heat transfer efficiency and easier cleaning. IPP stocks both shell and tube and plate heat exchangers. Contact IPP to discuss which design suits your application.
