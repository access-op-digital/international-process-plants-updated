Buying used boilers through International Process Plants gives chemical, food & beverage, pharmaceutical, power generation, and wastewater treatment buyers immediate access to steam, hot oil, firetube, and water tube boilers—from Cleaver Brooks, Ansaldo Volund, Chromalox, Bosch, Fulton, Burnham, Beel Industrial, Babcock Wanson, Clayton, Kewanee, and other OEM manufacturers.

Capacities range from compact 46.5 kg/h (102.5 lb/h) electric units to industrial 40,350 kg/h (89,000 lb/h) water tube steam boilers, with power ratings from 7.5 kW (10 HP) to 13,100 kW (17,550 HP) and pressure ratings from 1.03 bar (15 psi) to 91 bar (1,300 psi). Boiler types include fire-tube, water-tube, hot oil heaters, waste heat recovery boilers, and electric boilers.
