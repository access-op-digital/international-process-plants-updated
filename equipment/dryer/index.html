Browse the IMS inventory for current availability, or contact IPP to discuss your specific drying requirements.
Rotary vacuum dryers are indirect batch dryers that remove moisture under vacuum conditions, making them ideal for temperature-sensitive products, solvent recovery, and materials requiring precise drying control. Spray dryers convert liquids into dry powders by atomizing the feed into a hot air stream for rapid evaporation, making them faster and more suitable for liquids than rotary or tray dryers. Vacuum dryers operate at lower temperatures under reduced pressure, while spray dryers use high inlet temperatures with very short contact times.
IPP stocks dryers in stainless steel (SS 304, 316, 316L, 304L, austenitic grades), Hastelloy (C4), carbon steel, titanium, and glass-lined construction. Stainless steel is preferred for pharmaceutical, food, and sanitary applications. Hastelloy and specialty alloys serve corrosive or reactive environments. Carbon steel is used for general-purpose chemical processing.
