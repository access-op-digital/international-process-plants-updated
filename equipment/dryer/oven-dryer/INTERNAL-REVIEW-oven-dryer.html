Convection Drying Ovens vs Vacuum Ovens

A convection oven dries with circulating hot air at atmospheric pressure, while a vacuum oven dries in a sealed, evacuated chamber. The table sets the two designs side by side.

Factor Convection Oven Vacuum Oven
Heat Transfer Fans move heated air over the product (forced air convection) Heat reaches the product by conduction from heated shelves or walls and by radiation
Pressure Atmospheric Below atmospheric, 0.667 mbar (0.5 torr) operating vacuum on IPP's Barlow Whitney units
Atmosphere Heated air surrounds the product Little or no air remains in the chamber, which limits oxidation
Drying Temperature Set by the oven temperature Moisture and solvents evaporate at a lower temperature under vacuum
Vapor Handling Moist air leaves through vents or an exhaust A vacuum pump draws vapor out of the sealed chamber
