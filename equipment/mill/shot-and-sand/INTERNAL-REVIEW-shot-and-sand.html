Bead Mills vs Attritors

Both machines grind with agitated media, and media size, shaft speed and chamber design separate them.

Factor Bead Mill Attritor
Design A sealed horizontal or vertical chamber with discs or pins on a fast shaft A stationary tank with a central shaft whose arms stir the media
Media Small beads of 0.1 to 3 mm (0.004 to 0.12 in) Media of 1 mm (0.04 in) and up, including balls
Shaft Speed High-speed agitation Slow running, at circumferential speeds of 0.5 to 2 m/s (1.6 to 6.6 ft/s)
Operation Continuous pass or recirculation, with a screen or gap holding the beads Batch or continuous
