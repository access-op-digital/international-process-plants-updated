Glass lined parts
are critical components in industries that handle corrosive, high-purity or reactive materials. These parts—ranging from nozzles, valves, dip pipes, agitators, baffles, manway covers to main covers, valves, mechanical seals, drive systems, protection rings and gaskets—help preserve the integrity of reactors, vessels and dryers, and heat exchangers. With over
47 years of experience
,
UGE
offers used, surplus and secondhand glass lined parts from
20 complete plant sites
and maintains
15,000+ ready-to-ship systems and components
across the globe.
All glass lined parts stocked by UGE are carefully inspected for integrity and compatibility and come from top-tier OEMs like De Dietrich, Pfaudler and Cerreactor.
Primary uses across industries
In
pharmaceutical and biotech
applications, for maintaining product purity and preventing contamination
In
fine and specialty chemical processing
, where parts are exposed to aggressive solvents or acids
In
agrochemical manufacturing
, for batch processing and storage of caustic materials
In
flavor and fragrance
production, where product integrity is critical
Types of parts available
Glass lined nozzles, dip tubes and spargers
Agitator shafts and blades with glass lining
Manways, covers, baffles and thermowells
Valves, fittings, and full jacketed piping assemblies
Drive systems and gearboxes for reactors and agitated tanks
Mechanical seals for glass lined reactors
Common materials of construction
These parts are manufactured with corrosion resistance and chemical compatibility in mind:
Carbon steel or stainless steel
with fused glass enamel lining
High-performance gaskets and seals
for glass lined equipment
Precision-machined interfaces
for exact reactor or pipe fitting
Key features of glass lined parts
Chemically inert interior surface resists acids, bases and solvents
Smooth glass finish minimizes product hold-up and eases cleaning
OEM-matched designs for seamless integration with reactors and vessels
Ready-to-ship replacements reduce costly plant downtime
Compliant with cGMP and FDA standards for regulated production
