Inverting filter centrifuges feature a unique design where the filter cloth is mechanically inverted during discharge, gently removing solids without damaging fragile crystals or products. These machines are ideal for high-purity applications requiring low residual moisture, minimal contamination, and easy cleaning between batches.
Primary uses across industries
In
pharmaceutical production
, inverting filter centrifuges recover active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs) and delicate intermediates.
In
fine chemical manufacturing
, they separate and purify specialty chemicals and catalysts.
In
food and beverage processing
, they handle specialty starches, proteins, and sensitive food ingredients.
In
biotechnology
, they separate biomass or highly sensitive biological materials.
In
specialty materials industries
, they process fine powders and high-value solids needing gentle handling.
Types of materials processed
Fragile crystals or sensitive solids requiring gentle separation
Slurries with low solids concentration
Fine chemicals, APIs, biotech materials, and specialty food ingredients
Products requiring extremely low residual moisture after separation
Common materials of construction
Inverting filter centrifuges are built to meet sanitary, chemical, and high-purity standards:
Stainless steel
for food-grade, pharmaceutical, and general chemical applications
Hastelloy
for aggressive or highly corrosive chemical environments
Specialty alloys or coatings
for customized chemical compatibility
Key features of inverting filter centrifuges
Gentle solids discharge via mechanical cloth inversion
High recovery rates with minimal product breakage
CIP (clean-in-place) design for sanitary or multi-product operations
Fully enclosed design for containment and operator safety
Efficient washing and dewatering in a single operation
Centrifuge-Inverting Filter FAQ
Have questions about Inverting Filter Centrifuges or our services? Learn more about our products and
why customers choose IPP.
How does shipping work?
We offer several options for shipping, including internationally. Should the customer choose, they can arrange their own shipping.
What about used equipment? Where do you get it?
About 98% of our used equipment comes from plant-type liquidations in which we are required to not sell the systems or plants as a whole back into the marketplace. Our inventories come from those pieces that didn’t sell at the site but still have great value.
What industries rely most on inverting filter centrifuges?
Pharmaceutical, fine chemical, biotech, and specialty food industries rely heavily on inverting filter centrifuges for gentle, high-purity separation.
How do inverting filter centrifuges compare to traditional basket centrifuges?
Inverting filter centrifuges gently discharge solids by inverting the filter cloth, preserving delicate products, while traditional basket centrifuges typically rely on scraping or manual removal.
What maintenance is required for inverting filter centrifuges?
Regular inspection and maintenance of the filter cloth, mechanical inversion system, seals, and drive components ensures consistent performance and extended machine life.
