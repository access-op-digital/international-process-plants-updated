Basket centrifuges operate by spinning a perforated basket at high speeds, using centrifugal force to separate solids from liquids. They are essential for batch and continuous separation processes in industries ranging from pharmaceuticals to wastewater treatment. Their design allows for efficient handling of a wide variety of slurries, pastes, and particulate-laden liquids.
Primary uses across industries
In
pharmaceutical manufacturing
, basket centrifuges separate and dewater active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs) and intermediates.
In
chemical processing
, they recover fine chemicals, catalysts, and specialty powders.
In
food and beverage production
, basket centrifuges clarify juices, extract oils, and process dairy products.
In
wastewater treatment
, they remove suspended solids to purify effluent streams.
In
mining and mineral processing
, they dewater mineral slurries and separate valuable ores.
Types of materials processed
Slurries containing fine or coarse solids
Wet crystals requiring dewatering and washing
Viscous liquids and pastes needing solid-liquid separation
Heat-sensitive materials requiring gentle separation
Common materials of construction
Basket centrifuges are engineered to endure tough processing conditions while maintaining product purity:
Stainless steel

Hastelloy

Carbon steel

Specialty linings or coatings

Key features of basket centrifuges
High centrifugal forces for rapid and thorough separation
Perforated or imperforated basket options for different process needs
Designs for manual, semi-automatic, or fully automatic operation

What industries rely most on basket centrifuges?
Pharmaceutical, chemical, food, wastewater treatment, and mining industries rely on basket centrifuges for high-speed solid-liquid separation.=
How do basket centrifuges compare to decanter centrifuges?
Basket centrifuges are ideal for batch separation and dewatering of larger solids, while decanter centrifuges offer continuous separation for fine particulates.
What maintenance is required for basket centrifuges?
Routine inspection of baskets, bearings, seals, and drive systems—along with cleaning and balancing checks—ensures consistent performance and long service life.
