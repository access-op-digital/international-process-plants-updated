Solid bowl decanter centrifuges continuously separate solids from liquids by spinning a cylindrical bowl at high speeds, with an internal screw conveyor transporting solids toward the discharge end. These machines are ideal for handling slurries with moderate to high solids content and are essential for industries requiring high-throughput, continuous operation.

Key features of solid bowl decanter centrifuges
Continuous operation with automatic solids discharge
Adjustable differential speeds for optimal separation performance

What industries rely most on solid bowl decanter centrifuges?
Wastewater treatment, chemical manufacturing, oil and gas, food processing, and biotech industries depend on decanter centrifuges for continuous solid-liquid separation.
How do solid bowl decanter centrifuges compare to disc bowl centrifuges?
Solid bowl decanters are designed for slurries with higher solids content, while disc bowl centrifuges are better suited for fine particle and liquid-liquid separations at lower solids loads.
What maintenance is required for solid bowl decanter centrifuges?
Routine inspection and maintenance of bearings, seals, bowl surfaces, and drive systems ensure reliable performance and minimize downtime.
