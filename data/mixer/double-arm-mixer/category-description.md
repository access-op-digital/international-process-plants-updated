Used double arm mixers
are designed for blending high-viscosity materials that standard agitators can’t handle. Featuring two counter-rotating blades (typically sigma or Z-shaped), these mixers apply intense kneading and folding action—ideal for adhesives, rubbers, mastics, and heavy polymer compounds. With more than
47 years of process expertise
,
IPP
supplies used, surplus and secondhand double arm mixers from

deep inventory of 15,000+ process systems

IPP carries jacketed and non-jacketed double arm mixers from top manufacturers like Baker Perkins, Day, Kneader Machinery Co., Jaygo, and Littleford.
Applications and industries
In
rubber and elastomer production
, for compounding raw rubber with fillers and oils
In
adhesives and sealants
, for kneading viscous polymers and curing agents
In
plastics and resins
, for blending PVC, polyurethanes and thermoplastics
In
ceramics and refractories
, for wet mixing clays, powders, and pastes
Types of materials processed
Very high-viscosity compounds (1 million+ cps)
Semi-solids, mastics and dough-like mixtures
Reactive materials requiring uniform blending under controlled temperature
Solvent-based formulations in explosion-proof environments
Common materials of construction
These mixers must withstand torque, abrasion, and chemical exposure:
Hardened carbon steel
for rugged compounding applications
316L stainless steel
for food, pharma or corrosive blends
Chrome-plated or coated internals
for easy cleanout and non-stick performance
Jacketed chambers
for heating or cooling during mixing
Key features of double arm mixers
Sigma or Z-type counter-rotating blades for aggressive kneading
Heavy-duty gear or hydraulic drives for torque transmission
Split troughs for easier discharge and cleaning
Vacuum, pressure, or explosion-proof options available
PLC-controlled operation for precise timing and repeatability

Mixer-Double Arm FAQ
Have questions about Double Arm Mixers or our services? Learn more about our products and
why customers choose IPP.
What is the benefit of a sigma blade versus a Z blade?
Both blade types offer intensive kneading, but sigma blades are ideal for general heavy-duty use, while Z blades can offer more aggressive folding depending on the formulation.
Can double arm mixers be used under vacuum or pressure?
Yes – many are designed for vacuum degassing or pressure mixing and come with reinforced seals and drive systems.
What industries use double arm mixers most?
Rubber, adhesives, sealants, and plastics manufacturing all rely heavily on this equipment for their dense, hard-to-process materials.
