What’s the benefit of using a twin shell mixer over a ribbon or paddle mixer?
Twin shell mixers are gentler, with no internal agitators, making them ideal for fragile or segregative materials.
Can I add liquids to a twin shell mixer?
Some models feature intensifier bars or liquid spray options, though these mixers are primarily designed for dry materials.
Are these mixers available in sanitary or GMP configurations?
Yes – IPP offers 316L stainless steel models with polished interiors and CIP-ready access ports.
