Used continuous mixers
are designed to deliver consistent, uniform blending of materials in a nonstop flow. Ideal for high-volume processing, these mixers eliminate batch downtime while maintaining strict control over mixing quality and retention time. With more than
47 years of process industry experience
,
IPP
supplies used, surplus and secondhand continuous mixing systems from
20 global plant sites
and maintains a
deep inventory of 15,000+ ready-to-ship systems
.
IPP offers a range of continuous mixers from trusted manufacturers including Readco Kurimoto, Gericke, Littleford, Hosokawa, and Lödige.
Applications and industries
In
food processing
, for continuously mixing dough, cereals, protein powders and ready-to-eat products
In
chemical and plastics manufacturing
, for compounds, pigments, and resins that require uniform dispersion
In
pharmaceutical production
, for blending powders and granules with tight consistency control
In
building materials and adhesives
, for slurries, sealants and dry mortar
Types of materials processed
Free-flowing powders and granular blends
Slurries and pastes with medium to high viscosity
Moisture-sensitive compounds needing controlled exposure
Reactive materials requiring short residence times
Common materials of construction
Continuous mixers are engineered for durability, sanitation and process compatibility:
316L stainless steel
for hygienic and corrosion-resistant applications
Carbon steel or specialty alloys
for rugged or chemical-duty use
Jacketed housings and shafts
for heating, cooling or drying during mixing
Abrasion-resistant coatings or linings
for abrasive or high-solids formulations
Key features of continuous mixers
Precise control over residence time, shear and feed rate
Twin-shaft or paddle-style agitators for consistent dispersion
Integrated feeding and discharge systems for full automation
Customizable mixing zones for layering, wetting or heating
Available in lab, pilot and large-scale production capacities

Mixer-Continuous FAQ
Have questions about Continuous Mixers or our services? Learn more about our products and
why customers choose IPP.
What are the advantages of continuous mixing over batch mixing?
Continuous mixers offer consistent quality, reduced downtime, and better scalability for high-volume operations.
Can continuous mixers handle both wet and dry materials?
Yes – many models are designed to process powders, slurries and pastes with precise moisture and texture control.
How do I know if a continuous mixer is right for my process?
If your operation requires consistent output, short cycle times or high volumes, a continuous mixer may be ideal. IPP can help assess your needs.
