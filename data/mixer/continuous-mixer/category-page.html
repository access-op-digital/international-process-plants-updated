Used continuous mixers are designed to deliver consistent, uniform blending of materials in a nonstop flow. Ideal for high-volume processing, these mixers eliminate batch downtime while maintaining strict control over mixing quality and retention time. With more than 47 years of process industry experience, IPP supplies used, surplus and secondhand continuous mixing systems from 20 global plant sites and maintains a deep inventory of 15,000+ ready-to-ship systems.

IPP offers a range of continuous mixers from trusted manufacturers including Readco Kurimoto, Gericke, Littleford, Hosokawa, and Lödige.

Applications and industries

  • In food processing, for continuously mixing dough, cereals, protein powders and ready-to-eat products
  • In chemical and plastics manufacturing, for compounds, pigments, and resins that require uniform dispersion
  • In pharmaceutical production, for blending powders and granules with tight consistency control
  • In building materials and adhesives, for slurries, sealants and dry mortar

Types of materials processed

  • Free-flowing powders and granular blends
  • Slurries and pastes with medium to high viscosity
  • Moisture-sensitive compounds needing controlled exposure
  • Reactive materials requiring short residence times

Common materials of construction

Continuous mixers are engineered for durability, sanitation and process compatibility:

  • 316L stainless steel for hygienic and corrosion-resistant applications
  • Carbon steel or specialty alloys for rugged or chemical-duty use
  • Jacketed housings and shafts for heating, cooling or drying during mixing
  • Abrasion-resistant coatings or linings for abrasive or high-solids formulations

Key features of continuous mixers

  • Precise control over residence time, shear and feed rate
  • Twin-shaft or paddle-style agitators for consistent dispersion
  • Integrated feeding and discharge systems for full automation
  • Customizable mixing zones for layering, wetting or heating
  • Available in lab, pilot and large-scale production capacities
