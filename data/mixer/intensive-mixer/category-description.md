Used intensive mixers
deliver aggressive mixing action through high-speed rotors and close-clearance chamber designs. These mixers are ideal for compounding dense materials that require rapid shear, dispersion and homogenization—especially in rubber, polymer and chemical production. With more than
47 years of industry expertise
,
IPP
supplies used, surplus and secondhand intensive mixers from
20 global process sites
and maintains a
massive inventory of 15,000+ ready-to-ship systems
.
IPP carries intensive mixers from top-tier manufacturers such as Farrel, Banbury, Werner & Pfleiderer, Kobe, and HF Mixing Group.
Applications and industries
In
rubber processing
, for incorporating fillers, plasticizers and curatives into natural or synthetic rubber
In
plastics
, for compounding thermoplastics, PVC blends and engineered polymers
In
chemicals
, for high-speed mixing of pastes, waxes, resins and binders
In
battery and electronics manufacturing
, for slurry preparation and electrode compounds
Types of materials processed
High-viscosity compounds
Heat-sensitive or reactive mixtures
Powder/liquid combinations requiring high shear
Elastomers, adhesives and specialty formulations
Common materials of construction
To withstand the demands of intensive mixing, these systems are built from:
Carbon steel frames
with abrasion-resistant linings
Chrome-plated or hard-coated rotors and chambers
for wear resistance
316L stainless steel
for corrosive or sanitary applications
Jacketed construction
for heating or cooling as needed
Key features of intensive mixers
High-speed, close-clearance rotors for intense shearing and dispersion
Pneumatic or hydraulic pressure rams for consistent material compression
Vacuum or pressurized mixing chambers
Explosion-proof motors for volatile formulations
Integrated discharge systems for fast batch turnover

Mixer-Intensive FAQ
Have questions about Intensive Mixers or our services? Learn more about our products and
why customers choose IPP.
How is an intensive mixer different from a standard double arm or paddle mixer?
Intensive mixers operate at higher speeds with tighter clearances, delivering stronger shear and faster dispersion—ideal for dense or reactive materials.
Are these mixers suitable for temperature-sensitive products?
Yes – many are jacketed for thermal control, and mixing cycles are typically short to reduce heat buildup.
Can used intensive mixers be customized or retrofitted?
IPP offers equipment with upgrade options including new controls, vacuum systems and blade replacements to match your exact process.
