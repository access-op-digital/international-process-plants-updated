Used Nauta mixers are vertical, conical screw mixers designed to gently mix powders, pastes, and slurries while preserving particle integrity and preventing segregation. The combination of axial and radial flow creates a low-shear blending action that is ideal for fragile, segregative, or heat-sensitive materials. With more than 47 years of process expertise, IPP supplies used, secondhand and surplus Nauta mixers from 20 global process plant sites and maintains a world-class inventory of 15,000+ ready-to-ship systems.

IPP sources Nauta-type mixers from trusted manufacturers including Hosokawa Micron, Bolz-Summix, and De Dietrich Process Systems.

Applications and industries

  • In pharmaceuticals, for gentle blending of APIs, excipients and granulated formulations
  • In chemicals, for reactive powders and catalysts that require low heat and consistent mixing
  • In food and nutraceuticals, for seasoning blends, drink mixes, and supplements
  • In plastics and additives, for colorants, powders, and dry polymer compounds

Types of materials processed

  • Fine powders and granules with varied particle size
  • Slurries, pastes and cohesive materials
  • Fragile or friable solids that break under shear
  • Heat-sensitive blends that must be processed at ambient temperature

Common materials of construction

Nauta mixers are built for precision and clean operation:

  • 316L stainless steel for sanitary, corrosive or pharmaceutical-grade environments
  • Carbon steel for general-purpose industrial use
  • Polished interiors for non-stick performance and quick product changeover
  • Jacketed vessels for optional heating or cooling during processing

Key features of Nauta mixers

  • Conical design with vertical screw agitator for consistent axial and radial flow
  • Gentle mixing with minimal energy input and low heat generation
  • High mixing homogeneity, even with small ingredient ratios
  • Vacuum or pressure operation available for sensitive compounds
  • Easy access for cleaning and inspection
