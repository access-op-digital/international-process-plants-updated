Used Nauta mixers
are vertical, conical screw mixers designed to gently mix powders, pastes, and slurries while preserving particle integrity and preventing segregation. The combination of axial and radial flow creates a low-shear blending action that is ideal for fragile, segregative, or heat-sensitive materials. With more than
47 years of process expertise
,
IPP

Have questions about Nauta Mixers or our services? Learn more about our products and
why customers choose IPP.
What makes Nauta mixers ideal for fragile materials?
The slow, tumbling action preserves particle structure and prevents product damage, even in fragile, segregative blends.
Can Nauta mixers handle liquids or pastes?
Yes – many models are used for wet solids, slurries or cohesive pastes and offer jacketed options for temperature control.
Are Nauta mixers available with vacuum or pressure features?
Absolutely. IPP offers both vacuum-rated and pressure-rated conical mixers for sensitive processing environments.
