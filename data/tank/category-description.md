Used tanks
are essential in nearly every process industry — storing liquids, slurries, and raw materials with the flexibility to meet sanitation, pressure, and heating requirements. Whether you need jacketed mixing tanks for a pharma plant or bulk stainless storage tanks for a chemical facility,
IPP
offers
ready-to-ship surplus and secondhand tanks
from
20 global plant sites
. With over
47 years of experience
, IPP maintains the largest inventory of used process equipment in the world — more than 15,000 pieces in stock and available now.
IPP carries vertical and horizontal tanks, pressure vessels, agitator tanks, and sanitary tanks in multiple sizes and configurations. Manufacturers include Pfaudler, Feldmeier, Walker, DCI, and Mueller, with options to suit everything from food-grade to high-pressure chemical environments.

In
chemical manufacturing

Tank FAQ
Have questions about Tanks or our services? Learn more about our products and
why customers choose IPP.
What types of tanks does IPP carry?
IPP offers stainless steel, glass-lined, carbon steel, and poly tanks — with pressure ratings, jackets, and mixing options available.
Can I find tanks suitable for food or pharmaceutical use?
Yes. IPP stocks sanitary-grade tanks with polished finishes and clean-in-place (CIP) designs.
Are the tanks rated for high pressure or vacuum applications?
Many of our tanks are ASME-coded and rated for vacuum or pressurized service.
