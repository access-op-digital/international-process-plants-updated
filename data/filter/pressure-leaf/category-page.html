Used filters are essential components in countless process operations where solids must be separated from liquids. Whether you’re removing fine particulates, clarifying solutions, or recovering valuable materials, industrial filters play a critical role in improving product quality, protecting downstream equipment, and maintaining compliance. With more than 47 years of experience, IPP supplies used, secondhand, and surplus filters from 20 global plant sites and maintains an extensive inventory of 15,000+ process systems.

IPP stocks a wide range of filter types—pressure leaf, plate and frame, cartridge, vacuum drum, nutsche, and candle filters—from leading brands like Sparkler, Pall, Funda, and Millipore.

Primary uses across industries

  • In chemical processing, for separating solids from liquids in catalyst recovery, resin filtration, and acid clarification
  • In pharmaceuticals, for sterile filtration of intermediates, APIs, and final formulations
  • In food and beverage, for wine clarification, edible oil filtration, and sugar refining
  • In environmental and wastewater, for sludge dewatering and contaminant removal

Types of materials processed

  • Slurries, suspensions, and solutions with fine particulates
  • Heat-sensitive or viscous liquids requiring gentle handling
  • Solvent-based, aqueous, or corrosive mixtures

Common materials of construction

Industrial filters are built to resist corrosion, handle pressure, and maintain hygiene:

  • Stainless steel for sanitary, pharma and food-grade use
  • Carbon steel for robust chemical and utility processes
  • Hastelloy, titanium or lined steel for aggressive media or high-purity applications

Key features of process filters

  • Multiple filter formats to match flow rate and solids content
  • Jacketed options for temperature-sensitive operations
  • ASME code-rated vessels available for pressure applications
  • Options for automated cake discharge or backflushing
  • Compact, modular systems designed for integration and easy maintenance
