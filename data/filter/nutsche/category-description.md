Filter FAQ
Have questions about Filters or our services? Learn more about our products and
why customers choose IPP.
What’s the difference between pressure leaf and plate and frame filters?
Pressure leaf filters offer continuous, enclosed operation ideal for fine filtration, while plate and frame units are great for batch operations and easier cake discharge.
Are used filters suitable for pharmaceutical or food processing?
Yes – many are built to sanitary standards and can be revalidated or retrofitted with new filter media and gaskets.
What factors should I consider when selecting a filter?
Key factors include particle size, solids concentration, flow rate, operating pressure, temperature, and chemical compatibility.
