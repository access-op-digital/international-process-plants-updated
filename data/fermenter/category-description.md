### Block 2

Used fermenters are essential in biotechnology, pharmaceuticals, and food manufacturing, providing a sterile environment for microbial growth, enzymatic reactions, or cell culture processing. Designed for precise control of temperature, agitation, pH, and dissolved oxygen, fermenters are key to consistent batch quality and regulatory compliance. With more than 47 years of experience , IPP supplies used, surplus and secondhand fermenters from 20 global plant sites and maintains an unmatched inventory of 15,000+ process systems . 
 From stainless steel production-scale units to glass-lined pilot vessels, IPP’s inventory supports a wide range of fermentation applications—from antibiotics and biofuels to cultured proteins and enzymes. 
 Primary uses across industries 
 
 In pharmaceutical and biotech , for fermentation of antibiotics, vaccines, and therapeutic proteins 
 In nutraceuticals and food , for producing probiotics, amino acids, yeasts and flavor enhancers 
 In industrial biotech , for biosynthesis of enzymes, biofuels and organic acids 
 
 Types of materials processed 
 
 Microbial, bacterial and yeast cultures 
 Mammalian cell cultures in sterile environments 
 Media containing sugars, proteins, or specialized nutrients 
 
 Common materials of construction 
 Fermenters are engineered to meet cleanroom and cGMP requirements: 
 
 316L stainless steel for sanitary pharmaceutical and food-grade operations 
 Glass-lined steel for corrosion resistance in acidic or highly reactive media 
 Jacketed vessels for precise temperature control 
 
 Key features of industrial fermenters 
 
 CIP/SIP (clean-in-place / steam-in-place) systems for sterility 
 Integrated pH, DO, temperature and pressure control 
 Top- or bottom-mounted agitation with low-shear options 
 Sampling ports, spray balls and sterile air spargers 
 PLC automation and SCADA-ready systems for batch tracking
