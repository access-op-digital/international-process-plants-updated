Spray dryers convert liquids into dry powders by atomizing the feed into a hot air stream, allowing for fast, controlled evaporation. Ideal for heat-sensitive products, spray dryers are used in a wide range of industries to ensure consistent particle size, solubility and purity. With over 47 years of experience, IPP supplies surplus, secondhand and used spray dryers from 20 complete process plant sites and an unmatched inventory of more than 15,000 ready-to-ship systems.

Spray dryers can be configured for co-current, counter-current or mixed-flow operation and often feature cyclones, bag filters or scrubbers for product recovery and emission control.

Primary uses across industries

  • In pharmaceuticals and biotech, they dry active ingredients, enzymes, and injectables
  • In food and dairy, they process milk powders, proteins, flavors and instant beverages
  • In chemical manufacturing, they dry pigments, polymers, detergents and fine resins
  • In ceramics and materials science, they produce powders for advanced manufacturing

Types of materials processed

  • Liquid slurries and emulsions
  • Heat-sensitive solutions requiring fast drying
  • Products requiring controlled particle size and powder consistency

Common materials of construction

Spray dryers are built for cleanability, thermal resistance and corrosion protection:

  • Stainless steel for food, pharma and chemical processing
  • Hastelloy or specialty alloys for corrosive or reactive feeds
  • Sanitary finishes for cGMP environments and FDA compliance

Key features of spray dryers

  • Atomization for rapid surface drying
  • Adjustable inlet/outlet temperatures for thermal control
  • Multiple recovery systems (cyclones, bag filters, scrubbers)
  • Ideal for scale-up from pilot to production
  • Uniform particle size and high product yield
