Unlike direct-fired rotary dryers, steam tube dryers transfer heat through conduction via rotating tubes filled with steam. This approach protects sensitive materials while allowing continuous, high-capacity operation.

Dryer-Rotary Steam Tube FAQ
Have questions about Rotary Steam Tube Dryers or our services? Learn more about our products and
why customers choose IPP.
What’s the main advantage of steam tube dryers over direct rotary dryers?
Steam tube dryers offer gentle, uniform heating without direct flame exposure, ideal for temperature-sensitive or solvent-based materials.
What industries most often use steam tube dryers?
Pharma, fine chemicals, food processing, resins and any application requiring careful heat control or indirect drying.
How do I maintain a steam tube dryer?
Maintenance includes checking steam pressure and seals, inspecting the rotating shell and tube integrity, and cleaning to prevent fouling.
