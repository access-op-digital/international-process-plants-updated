Twin shell and double cone dryers
are rotating batch dryers used for low-shear, uniform drying of powders, granules and crystalline materials. Their simple, sealed design makes them ideal for vacuum drying and mixing sensitive or friable compounds. With over
47 years of experience
,
IPP
offers a large inventory of secondhand and surplus twin shell and double cone dryers from
20 complete plant sites
and more than
15,000 ready-to-ship systems
.
These dryers operate by rotating the vessel to gently tumble the product while applying indirect heat through a jacket. Vacuum can be applied to reduce drying temperature, which is especially valuable for thermally sensitive materials or solvent removal.
Primary uses across industries
In
pharmaceutical production
, they dry APIs, intermediates and fine powders without degrading the product
In
chemical manufacturing
, they process specialty powders, pigments and sensitive catalysts
In
nutraceuticals and food
, they’re used for vitamins, proteins, extracts and granular ingredients
In
plastics and resins
, they dry additives and heat-sensitive compounds
Types of materials processed
Heat-sensitive powders and granules
Free-flowing solids requiring gentle tumbling
Products containing residual solvents
Common materials of construction
These dryers are built for corrosion resistance, cleanability and safe operation under vacuum:
Stainless steel
for pharmaceutical and food-grade use
Carbon steel
for non-corrosive industrial products
Specialty alloys
like Hastelloy for reactive or high-purity applications
Key features of twin shell and double cone dryers
Gentle tumbling motion for uniform drying
Vacuum-compatible for low-temperature operation
Low shear – preserves fragile particles and blends
Sealed environment minimizes contamination
Easy to clean and maintain, with minimal moving parts
Dryer-Twin Shell & Double Cone FAQ
Have questions about Twin Shell & Double Cone Dryers or our services? Learn more about our products and
why customers choose IPP.
What’s the main difference between a twin shell and a double cone dryer?
Both function similarly, but twin shell dryers feature V-shaped vessels while double cone dryers have a symmetric conical design – the choice depends on capacity, layout and material flow needs.
Why are these dryers preferred for pharmaceuticals and food?
They offer clean, low-shear, enclosed drying that prevents cross-contamination and protects sensitive materials.
What maintenance is required?
Regular inspection of seals, vacuum connections, and vessel rotation systems is key to ensuring performance and product integrity.
