Synthetic Chemical Site Repurposing, Kirkby, UK

Kodak’s synthetic photographic chemicals complex, closed in a bankruptcy restructuring, became a multi-tenant industrial park.

  • 400,000 sq ftChemicals and engineering complex
  • 1stEnvironmental liability transfer in UK history
  • ~250People employed on site

The challenge

As part of its bankruptcy restructuring, Kodak needed to close and sell its 400,000 sq ft synthetic photographic chemicals and engineering complex in Kirkby, near Liverpool, quickly. The complex had employed nearly 500 people, and 100 remained at the time of shutdown.

Digital photography had also changed the synthetic photographic chemicals business dramatically.

International Process Plants’ solution

International Process Plants purchased the complex and took on its environmental liability. International Process Plants reports this as the first environmental liability transfer in the history of the United Kingdom, and it absolved Kodak of further responsibility for environmental cleanup.

The site’s flexible and profitable unit operations were then put to new use, and International Process Plants created an active industrial business park on the site.

The results

The site now operates as Knowsley Industrial Park. Tenants run new and existing units there, and the park employs approximately 250 people. Growth plans at the time pointed to approximately 200 additional jobs.

What this means for plant owners

A restructuring seller needs a clean exit fast. Taking on the environmental liability let the seller walk away, and the site’s unit operations found new tenants instead of standing idle.

International Process Plants service: Complete process plants

By reusing the existing site infrastructure, International Process Plants revitalizes dormant facilities so a new owner can get its product to market with less CAPEX and in significantly less time.
