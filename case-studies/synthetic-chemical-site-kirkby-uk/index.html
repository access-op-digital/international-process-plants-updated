Synthetic Chemical Site Repurposing, Kirkby, UK

A photographic chemicals complex closed in a bankruptcy restructuring became a multi-tenant industrial park.

  • 400,000 sq ftChemicals and engineering complex
  • 1stEnvironmental liability transfer in UK history
  • ~250People employed on site

The challenge

A bankruptcy restructuring required the owner of a 400,000 sq ft synthetic photographic chemicals and engineering complex to close and sell it quickly. The complex had employed nearly 500 people, and 100 remained at the time of shutdown.

Digital photography had also changed the synthetic photographic chemicals business dramatically.

IPP’s solution

IPP purchased the complex and took on its environmental liability. IPP reports this as the first environmental liability transfer in the history of the United Kingdom, and it absolved the restructuring company of further responsibility for environmental cleanup.

The site’s flexible unit operations were then put to new use, and IPP created an active industrial business park on the site.

The results

The site now operates as Knowsley Industrial Park. Tenants run new and existing units there, and the park employs approximately 250 people. Growth plans at the time pointed to approximately 200 additional jobs.

Units operated by park tenants

  • Solvent recovery plant
  • Silver recovery plant
  • Pilot plant

What this means for plant owners

A restructuring seller needs a clean exit fast. Taking on the environmental liability let the seller walk away, and the site’s unit operations found new tenants instead of standing idle.
