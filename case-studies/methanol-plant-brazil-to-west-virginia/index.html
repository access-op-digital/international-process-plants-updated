Methanol Plant Relocation, Brazil to West Virginia

A methanol plant idled by high gas costs in Brazil was dismantled and relocated for re-erection in West Virginia.

  • 750 tons/dayMethanol capacity
  • Brazil to USARelocation route
  • Over-the-fencePlanned supply to a major consumer

The challenge

A methanol plant in Brazil had just completed a turnaround that raised its maximum capacity to 750 tons/day. High natural gas costs in Brazil then forced the plant to shut down, leaving the recently upgraded unit idle.

IPP’s solution

IPP purchased the plant. Knowing that the US natural gas market was attractive for methanol production, IPP marketed it to companies and investors in the United States.

The plant was then dismantled in Brazil and relocated to West Virginia for re-erection.

The results

The plant is being re-erected in West Virginia, where it will provide methanol over-the-fence to a major consumer. The arrangement lets the company produce methanol locally with low-cost gas and avoid expensive freight charges.

What this means for plant owners

A plant that cannot compete on feedstock cost in one country can compete in another. IPP marketed this unit in the United States, where natural gas was attractive for methanol production.
