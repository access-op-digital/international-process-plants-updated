MIBK Solvent Plant Relocation, Deer Park, TX to India

A four-unit solvents complex left an operating refinery in Texas and now runs in southeastern India.

  • 208 million lb/yrComplex capacity
  • 4Production units
  • Texas to IndiaRelocation route

The challenge

IPP agreed to purchase and remove a solvents facility from a world-class refinery complex in Deer Park, Texas. The facility was a 208 million lb/yr M&H Plant complex built from four units.

Its equipment was intertwined with several units that stayed in operation, so the dismantling had to happen inside a working refinery.

IPP’s solution

After several months of evaluation and feasibility studies, IPP sold the M&H Plant to an IPP customer for relocation to India and took responsibility for dismantling and shipping.

IPP and its contractors worked under the refinery’s Environmental Health and Safety policies, which were stricter because of the live units around the equipment. The dismantling was surgical, to protect those operating units.

The results

The plant has been reinstalled in southeastern India. IPP reports that it is now the only MIBK plant in operation in the country.

Units in the M&H Plant complex

  • Methyl isobutyl carbinol (MIBC): 78 million lb/yr
  • Methyl isobutyl ketone (MIBK): 78 million lb/yr
  • Di-acetone alcohol (DAA): 34 million lb/yr
  • Hexylene glycol (HG): 18 million lb/yr

What this means for plant owners

A process unit can leave an operating site without shutting down the units around it. The MIBK project moved a complete complex out of a live refinery and into production on another continent.

IPP service: Process units and systems

IPP buys and sells individual process lines when only part of a plant becomes available for sale, or when the real estate is not available, and offers the line as a complete unit for relocation.

When a process unit shuts down, the owner often wants it removed to reduce carrying costs and make room for future expansion. Instead of demolition, the most costly option, IPP resells the unit as a usable second-hand system and weighs the value of the process equipment against the cost of demolition. IPP has saved individual clients millions of dollars by taking responsibility for site dismantling and clean-up, equipment removal and complete plant relocation.

IPP has purchased and relocated process units from virtually every major process manufacturing company and hundreds of medium-sized enterprises, from units inside active plant sites to complete plant closures. Most demolition projects cost the owner money; selling the plant for reuse yields a bottom-line profit, at a significantly higher value than scrap.
