Case studies: fact check for IPP

Internal review page, not for publishing. Each draft uses only figures from IPP’s corporate deck and the live case-studies page. These points need IPP to confirm before the pages go live.

All pages

  • Where these pages will live on internationalprocessplants.com (the live /case-studies/ page is one tabbed page with 4 of these projects).
  • Equipment links: the deck names no equipment for PET, Methanol or Nitric acid, so no equipment or listing links were added. Each page links to IPP’s plants or sell pages and to the other case studies.
  • Photos are cropped from the corporate deck slides. Original files would be sharper; please confirm they can be published.
  • Savings figures were left out: the deck says 60% in one place and 30-50% / 50-60% in another, and the live page says 50%.

PET Plant Repurposing, Darlington, SC

Sources: Deck slide 7; live /case-studies/ PET tab.

  • Restart: the deck says IPP "announced" a restart in 2016 and the jobs were "expected"; the live page says the plant was restarted in 2016 and "created at least 250 jobs". The page follows the live page. Confirm the restart and job count.
  • Carrying cost: deck "over $300,000 per month", live "$300,000/month". Page uses "more than $300,000".
  • Capacity basis: neither source gives a time unit for 550M lb / 250M lb. Pounds per year?
  • The live page says "low-cost cosmetic supplier"; the deck says "domestic". Page uses "domestic".
  • Year IPP bought the site, and who operates it now: not stated.
