Nitric Acid Plant Relocation, Pasadena, TX to India

A shut-down nitric acid plant made room for new installations in Texas and was sold to be relocated to India.

  • 500 tons/dayRated capacity
  • 65%Nitric acid concentration
  • 300 tons/dayTurn-down capability

The challenge

The previous owner of a nitric acid plant in Pasadena, Texas, exited the nitric acid business and shut the plant down. The site needed the space for new installations.

The plant was rated for 500 tons/day of 65% concentrated nitric acid, with turn-down capability as low as 300 tons/day.

IPP’s solution

IPP purchased the plant to make room for the new installations at the site. The plant was then offered as a complete unit for relocation.

The results

IPP sold the plant to a major global nitric acid manufacturer, to be relocated to India.

What this means for plant owners

A shut-down unit can leave a site as a complete plant. Selling it for relocation freed the space for new installations and placed the plant with a nitric acid manufacturer.
