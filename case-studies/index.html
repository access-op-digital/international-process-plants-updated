About International Process Plants

International Process Plants buys and sells second-hand industrial process plants, process systems and individual process equipment. Its portfolio holds more than 100 complete process plants, 15 complete industrial sites including the real estate, manufacturing assets and intellectual property, and more than 15,000 individual pieces of ready-to-ship process equipment. This inventory has made International Process Plants the world’s leading source of good quality, second-hand industrial process plants, process systems and individual process equipment.

International Process Plants is headquartered in Princeton Junction, New Jersey, between New York City and Philadelphia, and works through offices in 15 countries. Over the past 40 years its customer base has grown to 160,000 relationships, including all of the Fortune 100 members within the process industries and tens of thousands of small and midsize manufacturers.

International Process Plants’ tagline sums up the offer: Your Partner for Complete Plant Solutions.

International Process Plants’ three business areas

Complete process plants

International Process Plants buys and sells complete idled process plants, including the real estate, complete production systems, buildings and intellectual property, for operation in place or for relocation.

By reusing the existing site infrastructure, International Process Plants revitalizes dormant facilities so a new owner can get its product to market with less CAPEX and in significantly less time. International Process Plants purchases closed or idle manufacturing facilities, including the real estate, buildings, complete production systems and available intellectual assets, and may assume the environmental liabilities to remove the risk of additional cleanup costs for the seller.

Case studies: PET plant, Synthetic chemical complex, Oleochemical plant

Process units and systems

International Process Plants buys and sells individual process lines when only part of a plant becomes available for sale, or when the real estate is not available, and offers the line as a complete unit for relocation.

When a process unit shuts down, the owner often wants it removed to reduce carrying costs and make room for future expansion. Instead of demolition, the most costly option, International Process Plants resells the unit as a usable second-hand system and weighs the value of the process equipment against the cost of demolition. International Process Plants has saved individual clients millions of dollars by taking responsibility for site dismantling and clean-up, equipment removal and complete plant relocation.

International Process Plants has purchased and relocated process units from virtually every major process manufacturing company and hundreds of medium-sized enterprises, from units inside active plant sites to complete plant closures. Most demolition projects cost the owner money; selling the plant for reuse yields a bottom-line profit, at a significantly higher value than scrap.

Case studies: MIBK solvent plant, Methanol plant, Nitric acid plant

Process equipment

International Process Plants buys individual pieces of process equipment from customers worldwide, either surplus to ongoing operations or from the liquidation of its complete plants, and offers them at significant savings versus new, with immediate delivery.

The inventory covers second-hand, unused (from canceled projects), rebuilt and re-glassed equipment, much of it in exotic alloy, stainless steel and glass-lined construction. More than 15,000 pieces built by hundreds of OEMs are available from International Process Plants’ yards in the US, UK, Germany and Romania and from plant sites around the globe, including ASME coded, TÜV certified and National Board stamped equipment.

International Process Plants often ships equipment directly from its original plant site, so buyers can also relocate the ancillary equipment, such as valves, controls, piping and instrumentation, to further increase the value.

Reduce, reuse, recycle

When plants or equipment fall out of use at one manufacturer, they are traditionally scrapped, usually while they still have useful life left. International Process Plants’ mission is to reduce the need for new equipment by reusing existing equipment elsewhere in the world. That lowers the environmental impact and gets customers into production faster and at a lower cost.

Common questions about used plants and equipment

A used plant comes without a performance warranty. How do I know it will work?

International Process Plants buys and offers plants with documentation of their performance and operating condition before shutdown. Once the plant is dismantled, packed, shipped, reconditioned as required and reinstalled with consideration for climate differences, it will produce the same quantity and quality of product from the same inputs. International Process Plants considers this better than the 10% performance guarantee offered with a new plant.

Do second-hand plants and equipment have a limited useful life?

Most of International Process Plants’ equipment was operated by companies with aggressive maintenance programs, so even rotating equipment and equipment in heavy service was properly maintained. As long as the new owner continues the manufacturer’s required maintenance, the plants and equipment can have their useful lives extended.
