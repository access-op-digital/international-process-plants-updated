PET Plant Repurposing, Darlington, SC

A polyester fiber and PET resin site that cost its owner more than $300,000 a month to hold was set for a restart after IPP bought it.

  • $300,000+Monthly carrying cost taken over
  • 760 acresSite area
  • 2016Restart announced
  • 250+Jobs expected

The challenge

The Darlington site combined polyester fiber production with two resin lines: 550 million lb of capacity for PET bottle resin and 250 million lb for amorphous PET resin. The property covers 760 acres, with 1.1 million sq ft of covered manufacturing space and 100 acres inside the fence.

Holding the site cost the owner more than $300,000 every month, and the land carried environmental obligations on top of that cost.

IPP’s solution

IPP purchased the property and took over the carrying costs from the owner. The purchase moved the site’s environmental obligations to IPP as well, so the seller exited both the monthly expense and the cleanup exposure.

The restart plan built on the existing site infrastructure, local skilled labor and modernizations to improve efficiency.

The results

In 2016, IPP announced that the plant would restart as a fiber manufacturing facility to serve specialty industrial and healthcare demand for high-quality polyester fiber. The project was expected to create at least 250 jobs and bring a new, low-cost domestic supplier of polyester fiber to the market.

What this means for plant owners

An idle site keeps costing money every month. Selling the complete property to IPP, environmental obligations included, ended those costs for the owner and set the plant up for a restart.

IPP service: Complete process plants

By reusing the existing site infrastructure, IPP revitalizes dormant facilities so a new owner can get its product to market with less CAPEX and in significantly less time.
