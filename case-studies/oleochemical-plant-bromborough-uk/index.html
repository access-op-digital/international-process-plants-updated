How International Process Plants Turned a UK Oleochemical Site into an Award-Winning Waterfront Development

One site, two exits: the process units moved to Germany and the USA, and the land became a waterfront development.

  • 30 acresFatty acids facility
  • 2 countriesGermany and the USA
  • 2016Planning & Placemaking Awards winner

The challenge

A 30-acre fatty acids facility in Bromborough held esters, sorbitan, triacetin and glycerin purification plants. Feedstock for the oleochemicals industry comes mostly from Southeast Asia, so the plant was no longer viable to operate in the UK.

International Process Plants’ solution

International Process Plants purchased the facility and took over all carrying costs and environmental liabilities. The process units were relocated to Germany and the USA.

With the units gone, International Process Plants converted the site into a mixed-use commercial and residential waterfront development.

The results

The redevelopment won at The Planning & Placemaking Awards 2016. Combining existing, repurposed structures with new improvements, the site is becoming a place to live and work, with offices, homes and public riverfront space.

Plants on the original site

  • Esters plant
  • Sorbitan plant
  • Triacetin plant
  • Glycerin purification plant

What this means for plant owners

When a plant cannot run where it stands, its units and its land can each find a new use. International Process Plants moved the equipment to where it could operate and redeveloped the site it left behind.

International Process Plants service: Complete process plants

International Process Plants buys and sells complete idled process plants, including the real estate, complete production systems, buildings and intellectual property, for operation in place or for relocation.

By reusing the existing site infrastructure, International Process Plants revitalizes dormant facilities so a new owner can get its product to market with less CAPEX and in significantly less time. International Process Plants purchases closed or idle manufacturing facilities, including the real estate, buildings, complete production systems and available intellectual assets, and may assume the environmental liabilities to remove the risk of additional cleanup costs for the seller.
